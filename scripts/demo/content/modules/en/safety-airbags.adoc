.Front passenger airbag and rear-facing child seats
====
[#rear-facing-text]
An airbag that inflates in front of a rear-facing child seat can kill the child.

[#rear-facing-measures]
* [[rear-facing-m1]]Never fit a rear-facing child seat on the front passenger seat while the front passenger airbag is switched on.
* [[rear-facing-m2]]Switch the airbag off with the key switch in the glovebox. The indicator [.ui]##PASSENGER AIRBAG OFF## must light up.
====

[safety#position,severity=warning,symbols=w001]
.Sitting too close to an airbag
====
[#position-text]
An inflating airbag can injure people who sit too close to it or lean against the door.

[#position-measures]
* [[position-m1]]Keep at least 25 cm between your chest and the steering wheel.
* [[position-m2]]Never put objects, covers or stickers on the airbag covers.
====
