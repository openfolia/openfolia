= Replacing a fuse
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: fuse

[#intro]
If a function fails, check its fuse first. The fuses and what they protect are listed in xref:fuses.adoc[].

[#ref-off]
module::switch-off[]

[procedure#steps]
.Replace a fuse
. [[step-open]]Open the cover of the fuse box at the right end of the dashboard.
. [[step-pull]]Pull the fuse out with the plastic puller clipped inside the cover.
+
[#img-puller]
image::demo-image:fuse-puller["Plastic puller lifting a fuse out of the fuse box",width=45%]
. [[step-check]]A fuse with a broken wire has blown. Replace it with a fuse of the same rating.
. [[step-close]]Close the cover.

[variant#uk,when="market in (uk)"]
====
[#uk-text]
On right-hand drive vehicles, the fuse box is at the left end of the dashboard.
====
