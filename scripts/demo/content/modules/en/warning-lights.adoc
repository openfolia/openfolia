= Warning lights
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: reference
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: warning lights

[#intro]
A red light means: stop as soon as it is safe. A yellow light means: have it checked soon.

[#lights-table,cols="1,2",options=header]
.Warning lights and what to do
|===
|[[wl-h-light]]Light |[[wl-h-do]]What to do
|[[wl-brake]]Brake system (red) |[[wl-brake-do]]Stop. Check the brake fluid level. Do not drive on.
|[[wl-airbag]]Airbag (red) |[[wl-airbag-do]]Have the airbag system checked straight away.
|[[wl-tyre]]Tyre pressure (yellow) |[[wl-tyre-do]]Check the tyre pressures and correct them.
|[[wl-battery]]12 V battery (red) |[[wl-battery-do]]Stop and call roadside assistance.
|[[wl-power]]Reduced power (yellow) |[[wl-power-do]]Drive on carefully and have the vehicle checked.
|===
