= Refuelling and charging
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: refuelling, charging, energy

[#intro]
The filler flap or charging flap is on the left rear wing. Press its rear edge to open it.

[variant#petrol,when="drive in (petrol)"]
====
[procedure#refuel]
.Refuel the vehicle
. [[refuel-off]]Switch the engine off.
. [[refuel-flap]]Open the filler flap and turn the filler cap anticlockwise.
. [[refuel-fill]]Fill up with unleaded petrol of at least 95 RON (E10 is allowed) until the nozzle shuts off.
. [[refuel-close]]Turn the filler cap until it clicks and close the flap.
====

[variant#electric,when="drive in (electric)"]
====
[procedure#charge]
.Charge the vehicle
. [[charge-flap]]Open the charging flap.
+
[#img-port]
image::demo-image:charging-port["Charging socket behind the open flap, with the light ring around it",width=45%]
. [[charge-plug]]Plug in the charging cable until it locks. The light ring around the socket flashes green while the battery charges.
. [[charge-end]]To stop charging, unlock the vehicle and pull the plug out.

[#charge-times]
At a wall box with 11 kW the battery charges from 10 % to 80 % in about 4 hours; at a fast charger with 100 kW in about 30 minutes.
====
