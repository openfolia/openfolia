= Changing the brake fluid
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: technician
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: brakes, brake fluid

[#intro]
Brake fluid absorbs water over time, which lowers its boiling point. Change it every two years.

[procedure#steps]
.Change the brake fluid
. [[step-reservoir]]Draw off the old brake fluid from the reservoir under the bonnet.
. [[step-fill]]Fill the reservoir with new brake fluid DOT 4 up to the MAX mark.
. [[step-bleed]]Bleed each wheel brake in turn, starting at the rear right, until clear fluid comes out without bubbles.
. [[step-check]]Fill up to the MAX mark and check the pedal travel before the test drive.
