.Children without a suitable child seat
====
[#unsecured-text]
A seat belt alone does not protect a small child in an accident.

[#unsecured-measures]
* [[unsecured-m1]]Use a child seat that suits the child's height and weight.
* [[unsecured-m2]]Fix the child seat to the [.term.child-seat-anchorage]##child seat anchorage## or with the seat belt, as its own instructions say.
* [[unsecured-m3]]Never leave children alone in the vehicle.
====
