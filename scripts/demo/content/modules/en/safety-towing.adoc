[safety#jack,severity=danger,symbols=w001]
.Vehicle falling off the jack
====
[#jack-text]
A vehicle on a jack can slip and crush anyone underneath it.

[#jack-measures]
* [[jack-m1]]Never lie or reach under a vehicle that stands only on the jack.
* [[jack-m2]]Use only the [.term.jacking-point]##jacking points## marked on the sill.
====

[safety#hot-brakes,severity=caution,symbols=w017]
.Hot brakes and exhaust
====
[#hot-brakes-text]
After driving, brake discs and, on the petrol model, the exhaust can be hot enough to burn skin.

[#hot-brakes-measures]
* [[hot-brakes-m1]]Let the wheels cool down before you touch the brakes.
====
