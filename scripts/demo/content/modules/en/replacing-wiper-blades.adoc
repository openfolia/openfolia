= Replacing the wiper blades
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: wipers

[#intro]
Replace the wiper blades once a year, or as soon as they leave streaks.

[procedure#steps]
.Replace a wiper blade
. [[step-service]]Within ten seconds of switching the vehicle off, push the wiper lever down: the wipers move to the service position.
. [[step-lift]]Lift the wiper arm away from the windscreen.
. [[step-release]]Press the release button and pull the blade off the arm.
+
[#img-wiper]
image::demo-image:wiper-blade["Wiper arm lifted, release button pressed, blade sliding off",width=45%]
. [[step-fit]]Push the new blade onto the arm until it clicks, and fold the arm back.
