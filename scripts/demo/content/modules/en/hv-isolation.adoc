= Making the high-voltage system safe
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: technician
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: high voltage, isolation

[#intro]
Isolate the high-voltage system before any work on high-voltage parts, and before work near them that uses heat or cutting tools.

[procedure#steps]
.Isolate the high-voltage system
. [[step-off]]Switch the vehicle off and keep the key at least 5 m away.
. [[step-12v]]Disconnect the negative terminal of the 12 V battery.
. [[step-plug]]Pull the orange service plug under the rear seat and lock it away.
. [[step-wait]]Wait 5 minutes, then check with a two-pole tester that the system is free of voltage.
