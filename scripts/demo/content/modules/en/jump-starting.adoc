= Jump-starting
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: battery, jump start

[#intro]
If the 12 V battery is flat, the vehicle does not start. You can start it with jump leads from another vehicle.

[variant#petrol,when="drive in (petrol)"]
====
[#petrol-text]
The 12 V battery is in the engine compartment, on the left. Connect the jump leads directly to it.
====

[variant#electric,when="drive in (electric)"]
====
[#electric-text]
The electric model has a 12 V battery too: it switches the high-voltage system on. Connect the jump leads only to the two jump-start points under the bonnet, never to the high-voltage system.
====

[procedure#steps]
.Jump-start the vehicle
. [[step-red]]Connect the red lead to the positive terminal or jump-start point of your vehicle, then to the positive terminal of the other battery.
. [[step-black]]Connect the black lead to the negative terminal of the other battery, then to the negative jump-start point or to bare metal of your vehicle, away from the battery.
+
[#img-leads]
image::demo-image:jump-leads["Two batteries joined by a red lead between the positive terminals and a black lead between the negative ones",width=45%]
. [[step-start]]Start the other vehicle, then yours.
. [[step-remove]]Remove the leads in the reverse order.
