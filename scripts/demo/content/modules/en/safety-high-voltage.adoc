.Electric shock from the high-voltage system
====
[#hv-shock-text]
The [.term.high-voltage-system]##high-voltage system## works at up to 400 V. Touching its parts can cause fatal injuries.

[#hv-shock-measures]
* [[hv-shock-m1]]Never open, remove or repair parts with orange cables or orange covers.
* [[hv-shock-m2]]After an accident, switch the vehicle off and keep away from damaged high-voltage parts.
* [[hv-shock-m3]]Leave all work on the high-voltage system to a qualified workshop.
====
