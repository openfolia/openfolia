[#intro]
Service the vehicle at whichever interval comes first.

[#table,cols="2,1",options=header]
.Service schedule
|===
|[[ss-h-work]]Work |[[ss-h-interval]]Interval
|[[ss-inspection]]Inspection with fault memory read-out |[[ss-inspection-i]]Every year or 20,000 km
|[[ss-brake]]Change the brake fluid |[[ss-brake-i]]Every 2 years
|[[ss-filter]]Replace the pollen filter |[[ss-filter-i]]Every year
|[[ss-wipers]]Replace the wiper blades |[[ss-wipers-i]]Every year
|===

[variant#petrol,when="drive in (petrol)"]
====
[#petrol-text]
Petrol model: change the engine oil and its filter every year or 20,000 km, and the spark plugs every 60,000 km.
====

[variant#electric,when="drive in (electric)"]
====
[#electric-text]
Electric model: check the high-voltage system and the coolant of the battery every 2 years.
====
