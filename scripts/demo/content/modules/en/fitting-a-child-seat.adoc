= Fitting a child seat
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: child seat

[#intro]
Fit child seats on the rear outer seats. Read the safety notices on child seats and the child seat's own instructions first.

[procedure#steps]
.Fit a child seat to the anchorage
. [[step-find]]Find the two anchorage points between the seat cushion and the backrest of a rear outer seat.
. [[step-click]]Push the child seat's locking arms onto the anchorage points until both click.
+
[#img-anchorage]
image::demo-image:child-seat-anchorage["Child seat with its locking arm pushed onto the anchorage between seat cushion and backrest",width=45%]
. [[step-tether]]Fasten the top tether to the ring behind the backrest and tighten it.
. [[step-check]]Pull the child seat firmly: it must not come loose.
