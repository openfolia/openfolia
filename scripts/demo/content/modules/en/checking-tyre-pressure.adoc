= Checking the tyre pressure
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: tyres, tyre pressure

[#intro]
Check the tyre pressure with cold tyres, at the latest every two weeks. The right values are in xref:tyre-pressures.adoc[] and on the label in the driver's door frame.

[procedure#steps]
.Check the tyre pressure
. [[step-cap]]Unscrew the valve cap.
. [[step-gauge]]Press the tyre gauge onto the valve and read the pressure.
. [[step-adjust]]Add or let out air until the pressure is right.
. [[step-close]]Screw the valve cap back on.
. [[step-reset]]After a change, confirm the new pressure in the centre display under [.ui]##Vehicle## › [.ui]##Tyre pressure##.
