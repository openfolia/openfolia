= Warn other road users
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: snippet
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna

[variant#eu,when="market in (eu)"]
====
[#text]
Put on a high-visibility vest and set up the [.term.warning-triangle]##warning triangle## at least 50 m behind the vehicle, or 100 m on a motorway.
====

[variant#uk,when="market in (uk)"]
====
[#uk-text]
Put on a high-visibility vest. On a motorway, set up no [.term.warning-triangle]##warning triangle##: leave the vehicle and wait behind the safety barrier. Elsewhere, set it up at least 45 m behind the vehicle.
====
