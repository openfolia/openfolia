= Setting the clock
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: clock, time, display

[#intro]
The vehicle sets the clock automatically from the navigation signal. You can also set it by hand.

[procedure#steps]
.Set the clock
. [[step-menu]]In the centre display, open [.ui]##Settings## › [.ui]##Date and time##.
. [[step-auto]]Switch [.ui]##Automatic time## off.
. [[step-set]]Set the hours and minutes with the arrows, then tap [.ui]##Save##.

[NOTE#note-auto]
====
[#note-auto-text]
With automatic time on, the clock also changes to summer time and back by itself.
====
