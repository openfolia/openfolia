= Towing the vehicle
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: towing

[#intro]
The towing eye is with the jack under the floor of the load space.

[variant#electric,when="drive in (electric)"]
====
[#electric-text]
Tow the electric model only with all four wheels off the ground, on a transporter. Towed on its wheels, the motor makes a voltage that can damage the vehicle.
====

[procedure#steps]
.Fit the towing eye
. [[step-cover]]Press the cover in the bumper at its mark until it comes out.
. [[step-screw]]Screw the towing eye in anticlockwise, as far as it goes.
. [[step-neutral]]Switch the vehicle on and select [.ui]##N##.
