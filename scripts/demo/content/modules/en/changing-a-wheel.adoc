= Changing a wheel
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: flat tyre, wheel, jack

[#intro]
The spare wheel, the jack and the wheel brace are under the floor of the load space.

[#ref-secure]
module::secure-vehicle[]

[#ref-warn]
module::warning-triangle[]

[procedure#steps]
.Change the wheel
. [[step-loosen]]Loosen the [.term.wheel-bolt]##wheel bolts## by one turn while the wheel is still on the ground.
. [[step-jack]]Place the jack under the [.term.jacking-point]##jacking point## nearest the wheel and raise the vehicle until the wheel is clear of the ground.
+
[#img-jack]
image::demo-image:wheel-change["Jack under the jacking point on the sill, wheel lifted clear of the ground",width=60%]
. [[step-remove]]Remove the wheel bolts and take the wheel off.
. [[step-fit]]Fit the spare wheel and screw in the wheel bolts by hand.
. [[step-lower]]Lower the vehicle and tighten the wheel bolts crosswise.

[#after]
Check the pressure of the spare wheel as soon as you can, and have the damaged tyre repaired or replaced.
