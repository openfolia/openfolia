= Starting and switching off
:schema-version: 1
:module-type: procedure
:lang: en
:audience: owner
:facet-product: fenna
:keywords: start, ignition

[#intro]
The vehicle starts with the key in your pocket.

[procedure#start]
.Start the vehicle
. [[start-brake]]Press the brake pedal.
. [[start-button]]Press the [.ui]##START/STOP## button.
+
[#start-ready]
The petrol engine starts. On the electric model, the display shows [.ui]##READY##.
. [[start-gear]]Select [.ui]##D## or [.ui]##R## and release the parking brake.

[procedure#stop]
.Switch the vehicle off
. [[stop-park]]Stop and select [.ui]##P##.
. [[stop-brake]]Apply the [.term.parking-brake]##parking brake##.
. [[stop-off]]Press the [.ui]##START/STOP## button.
