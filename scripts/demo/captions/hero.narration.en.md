# Hero: voice-over (English)

> Spoken, calm; one story, its sentences running across the cuts: a reader's question the manual
> can't answer, the fix written once, the same question answered everywhere. Neutral: names,
> no role nouns, no "he" or "she". It leads into the end card and never repeats the cards' line.

## 01-question

- a: Someone changing a wheel wants to know one thing: how tight do the bolts go?
- b: The manual doesn't say.
- c: And an AI assistant can only repeat the gap.

## 02-idea

- a: That gap is in two manuals, every variant, three languages: eighteen PDFs.
- b: In OpenFolia, nothing is copied. Each piece is a module, written once,
- c: and manuals are built from modules, with variants choosing what each car gets.

## 03-fix

- a: So the fix goes in once.
- b: Mira adds the torque, right in the step.
- c: Both manuals that use it will pick it up.
- d: The German follows in the same change,
- e: the French goes to its translators.

## 04-review

- a: Jonas reads exactly what changed, and approves.

## 05-release

- a: With the French back, Ada creates release 1.2.
- b: All thirty files are built again.

## 06-answer

- a: And now the number is in every PDF.
- b: The same question on the portal finds its answer,
- c: and so does the assistant, quoting release 1.2.

## 07-close

- a: Fixed once. Right everywhere.
