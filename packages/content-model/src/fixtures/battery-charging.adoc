[#intro]
The {product-name} charges automatically at the charging station. Charge manually only after a deep discharge.

[safety#warn-voltage,severity=warning,symbols=w012]
.Electrical voltage at the charging contacts
====
[#warn-voltage-consequence]
Touching live contacts can cause burns.

[#warn-voltage-measures]
* [[warn-voltage-m1]]Switch off the charger before you touch the contacts.
* [[warn-voltage-m2]]Wear insulating gloves.
====
