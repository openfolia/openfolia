You change content only inside a revision. A revision collects your edits until it is reviewed
and merged. Nobody else reads them before that.

## Start a revision

1. Open the revision menu at the top right.
2. Choose **New revision**.
3. Enter a **Title**: what you are going to change.
4. Choose **Start**.

![The revision menu with Published content and New revision](/images/guide/en/revision-menu.webp)

The menu now shows your revision, and every page reads from it. A module you open in it is locked
for you, so nobody else changes it at the same time.

## Switch to another revision

1. Open the revision menu.
2. Type in **Search revisions** to find one, or pick it from the list. Each shows its state:
   **Draft**, **In review** or **Approved**.

Choose **Published content** to read what everyone reads again. Your revision keeps its changes.

You can also switch from the search palette: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and choose **Switch
revision**.

## Rename a revision

1. Switch to the revision.
2. Open the revision menu and choose **Rename revision**.
3. Change the **Title**, and add a **Description**: what it changes and why.
4. Choose **Save**.

## What happens next

- [Submit it for review](/guide/getting-started/first-edit) when your changes
  are done.
- [Update it from main](/guide/change-sets/update-from-main) when others merged changes meanwhile.
