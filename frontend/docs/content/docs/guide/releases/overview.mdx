A release is a fixed snapshot of the published content for one product version. It tags one
commit of the published content and renders every manual into PDF files. A release can be
withdrawn later, but never changed.

## The releases list

Choose **Releases** in the sidebar. The list shows every release, newest first, withdrawn ones
too: its **Product**, its **Product version**, how far its **Files** are, its **Status** and who
published it when. Narrow it with the **Product** and **Status** filters at the top.

Choose a row to see the release beside the list: who published it, its first files to download,
**Files and changes** for its page, and **Withdraw release**.

![The releases list with a release chosen and its panel beside the list](/images/guide/en/release-list.webp)

## Cut a release

You need a role that may publish. Then:

1. Choose **New release**. The **Create release** dialog has four steps.
2. **Product and version:** choose the **Product** and enter the **Product version**, for example
   `2.1`. The dialog names the commit the release will tag.
3. Choose **Next**. **Checks** lists every manual's checks at that commit. Fix each blocking issue
   first: **Fix** opens the place. Warnings don't hold the release back, but you confirm them by
   ticking **Release despite 55 warnings** (with your count).
4. Choose **Next**. **Outputs** lists the languages and manuals the release renders, and how
   each language's translations stand.
5. Choose **Next**. **Notes** takes a **Summary** for readers and, if you like, summaries per
   language. Turn on **Major change** to notify the readers of the manuals it changes. See
   [Release notes and changes](/guide/releases/release-notes).
6. Choose **Create release**.

![The Create release dialog on its first step, with the product and the product version](/images/guide/en/release-new.webp)

The banner above every step says how the checks stand, for example **55 warnings, nothing
blocking**. While a blocking issue is open, **Create release** stays disabled and says why.

![The Checks step: each manual's warnings with Fix, and the confirmation below](/images/guide/en/release-new-checks.webp)

A product version can be released once. A version that has a release already is refused in the
dialog.

## The release's page

Once created, the release's page opens: its name, **Published**, who cut it, its commit and its
tag. It has three parts:

- **Notes:** the summary, per language. See [Release notes and changes](/guide/releases/release-notes).
- **Changes:** what changed since the product's previous release.
- **Files:** every PDF, as it renders. See [Release files](/guide/releases/outputs).

![A release's page: the notes, the changes and the files](/images/guide/en/release-page.webp)

**All releases** goes back to the list.
