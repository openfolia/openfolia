A page design brings its logo, colours and fonts. A manual brings its cover image, which the
design shows on the title page. Change both in a draft revision of your own.

## Logo and colours

In the design (**Admin**, **Page design**, then the design):

- **Logo:** choose **Choose** to pick an image from the media library or upload one, or **Use the
  platform logo**. A second logo **On dark backgrounds** is used on coloured areas.
- **Colours:** **Primary**, **Accent** and **Text**, as `#rrggbb` or with the picker. Each says
  its contrast on white, and warns when it is too low for text.

## Fonts

![The Fonts section: the body and headings fonts, and Upload fonts](/images/guide/en/page-design-fonts.webp)

1. In **Fonts**, choose **Upload fonts** and pick the font files: TrueType, OpenType, WOFF or
   WOFF2. A file that is no font is refused and the message says why.
2. Under **Body** and **Headings**, choose the font. **The standard font** is the default.
3. Check the styles each font brings (**Regular**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Bold italic**) and its
   copyright and licence shown below it.
4. Tick **The fonts' licence allows embedding them in PDFs**. Until you do, the design reports a
   problem.

The preview and the PDF then use the fonts. The fonts are saved in the revision with the design.
If a design names a font whose file is not there, the PDF uses the standard font, and the
manual's checks report **Page design font missing**. Your administrator sets the largest font
file allowed.

## A manual's cover image

The **Image-led** layout shows a large cover image, and every layout shows one when a manual has
it. Without one, the layout closes up, with no empty area.

1. Open the manual: **Manuals** in the sidebar, then the manual.
2. In its **Settings** card, choose **Edit settings**.
3. Under **Cover image**, choose **Choose image** in the **All variants** row.
4. Pick an image from the media library, or upload one.
5. Fill in **Alternative text (English)**: what the image shows, for screen readers. It is
   required in the source language; other languages can have their own.
6. Choose **Use image**.

![The cover image settings: all variants, and each variant](/images/guide/en/page-design-cover-image.webp)

To give a variant a different image, use **Choose image** in that variant's row: its own image
replaces the manual's. A row with none says where its image comes from, for example **The
design's image, if any**.

The media library lists the image as used by the manual, and it cannot be deleted while a manual
names it. A cover image that is missing is a check finding, not a broken PDF.
