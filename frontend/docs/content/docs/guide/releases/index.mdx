title: 'Releases and PDFs'
description: 'Cutting a release, its files, page designs and stable addresses.'
articleId: 'guide.releases'
---

Cut a release for a product version, follow and download its PDF files, decide how they look, and
give readers addresses and QR codes that keep working.

A release pins all content at one commit: the modules, manuals, settings, page designs and terms.
See [Almost everything is content](/guide/getting-started/content).
