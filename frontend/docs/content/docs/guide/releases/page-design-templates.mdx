An own template lays a manual's PDF out with Typst files of your own in place of the layout's
(see [Page designs](/guide/releases/page-designs#own-templates)). Its files are content: you
change them in a revision of your own, in the editor or by pushing them to the content
repository, and they apply once the revision is merged.

## Open a template

1. Start or choose a draft revision of your own.
2. Choose **Admin** in the sidebar, then **Page design**.
3. Under **Own templates**, choose the template. From a design that uses it, **Edit the
   template's files** next to **Template** opens it too.

The screen shows the template's files on the left, the file you chose in the middle and a live
preview on the right. Changing a template needs a role that may edit all content, as changing a
page design does; everyone else sees it read-only, with why.

## Start a new template

1. Under **Own templates**, choose **New template**.
2. Give it a name: lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, such as `house-style`. It becomes the
   folder `templates/<name>/`.
3. Choose **Create**. The new template starts as a copy of the example template, the **Classic**
   layout as files of its own: `manual.typ`, `design.typ` and `default.typ`. It prints exactly
   what **Classic** prints until you change it.

To use it, choose it under **Template** in a page design (**Layout**).

## Edit a file

Choose a file in the list. The code editor colours Typst (and YAML, JSON and SVG) and numbers
the lines.

- **Tab** indents the line or the selected lines, **Shift+Tab** takes the indent back, and
  **Enter** keeps the line's indent. To leave the editor with the keyboard, press **Escape** and
  then **Tab**.
- What you type is saved in your revision shortly after you stop, and the preview updates.
  **Ctrl+S** (**Cmd+S** on a Mac) makes it a save point of the revision at once; this also
  happens after a short pause and when you leave the screen.
- A dot in the list marks the files your revision changed.
- Images and fonts in the template's folder are listed but not edited here: push them to the
  content repository.

A file holds at most 256 KiB of text. A larger one is not saved until it is smaller again.

## Add or delete a file

- **New file** asks for its path in the template's folder, such as `parts/notice.typ`. A path
  has folders and a name of letters, digits, `_`, `.` and `-`, at most 8 names deep (the file's own included) and 150
  characters long, and ends with a text extension: `.typ`, `.yaml`, `.yml`, `.json`, `.toml`,
  `.csv`, `.txt`, `.xml`, `.svg` or `.bib`.
- **Delete** removes the file open from the template in your revision. `manual.typ`, the
  template's entry, cannot be deleted.

## The live preview

The preview lays out a small manual that uses every function a template defines: the title page,
running text with each inline mark, every safety severity and admonition, steps, a list, a
table, a section, an FAQ entry. Step through its pages with the arrows. By default it uses the
settings of the first design that names the template (else the built-in design's); choose
another design under **Preview with**.

## When the template has an error

When the template does not compile, the preview turns grey and the problems are listed under the
code: the file, line and column, and the compiler's message. The lines are marked in the
editor, and the file in the list. Choose a problem to open its file at its line.

The revision's checks report **Page design template does not compile** at each design that names
the template, and the revision cannot be merged until the template is fixed. Until then the
published template stays in use.

A template reads only its own folder's files and the design's files, never the server's files,
another template or the network. A template that runs too long or needs too much memory is
stopped, and the preview says so as its problem.
