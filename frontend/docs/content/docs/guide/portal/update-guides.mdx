An update guide is a manual of steps, each tagged with the version that needs it. In the portal a
reader does not read it front to back: they say which version they are on and which one they are
going to, and the page shows exactly the steps for that jump, as a checklist.

![An update guide in the portal: the pickers beside the page, the steps of the update grouped before, during and after it, two ticked off](/images/guide/en/portal-update-guide.webp)

## Finding it

An update guide is listed with its product's manuals on the start page and in **All manuals**,
marked **Update guide**. It has one page per language, at
`https://<team>.openfolia.com/<language>/<product>/<guide>/` on a portal your provider hosts, or
under your own address on a portal you host yourself.

## Picking the update

The panel **Your update** (beside the page on a wide screen, above the steps on a phone) holds:

- **From version** and **To version**: the version installed now and the one you are going to.
  **To version** offers only the versions after **From version**. The page opens on the update from
  the second-newest version to the newest.
- **Level**: whose steps to show, from the audience levels the guide is written for (for example
  customers, or your service staff as well). It shows only when the guide offers more than one. A
  step written for a level shows for that level and the more restricted ones.
- **Your setup**: the options the steps depend on, for example whether the product runs as a
  cluster, each a list of its values. Until you pick a value, only the steps for every setup show.

The steps change as you pick, without reloading the page.

## The checklist

- The steps are grouped **Before you update**, **During the update** and **After the update**.
  Within each group they are in version order, and each step shows the version that brought it as
  a small tag.
- The line above the steps says how many there are ("12 steps"); screen readers announce it when
  it changes.
- Tick a step off when it is done. The ticks are kept in your browser for this guide and this
  range (from and to): close the page, come back later and they are still there. Another browser
  or device has its own. **Reset** clears the ticks of the range shown.
- When nothing applies ("Nothing to change between 3.0 and 3.1 for this setup"), the page says so.
- Printing prints the steps of the range shown, with boxes to tick on paper, without the panel.

## Sharing the update

The page's address holds your choice, for example
`…/software-update/?from=2.0&to=4.0&level=internal&opt=cluster:yes`. Send it, and the reader opens the
same update. An address that asks for a version, level or option the guide does not have opens the
default update, with a short note saying so. The ticks are not part of the address.

## Search

Search finds the steps of an update guide like the sections of any manual. A step opens the guide
on the update that ends at the step's version, at the step's level and with the setup its condition names, so the step is in the list.

## Without JavaScript

Without JavaScript the page shows the whole guide, version by version, each with its steps before,
during and after the update, and a note that picking needs JavaScript.
