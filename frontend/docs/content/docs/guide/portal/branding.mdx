The portal looks like your organisation, not like OpenFolia. At build time it reads the branding an
admin set in [Branding](/guide/admin/branding) and puts it on every page, in both languages.

## What it takes

- **The name**, in the browser tab and the page head.
- **The logos**, the light one on light pages and the one for dark pages on dark pages. Without a
  dark logo, the light one shows there too.
- **The favicon**.
- **The colours**: the primary colour for links, buttons and the focus ring, with a lighter shade
  of it on dark pages, and the accent.

## The contrast rule

A colour applies only when every contrast check passes, on light and on dark pages. A colour
that does not is left out and the page keeps its default, so readers always get text they can read.
The safety and status colours never change with a brand: a warning stays a warning.

## Rebuild after a change

The portal is static, so it keeps the branding it was built with. After you change the branding,
build the portal again and publish the new files. Readers see the change when it is hosted.

To build with OpenFolia's default look instead, set `CCMS_PORTAL_BRANDING=off` (see
[Build and preview](/guide/portal/build)). A portal also has that look when no branding is set.
