A revision goes from **Draft** to **In review** to **Approved**, and a publisher then merges it into
main, the published content. Each step is a button on the right of the **Review** page. You see
only the buttons your role may use; a greyed-out one says why when you point at it.

![A revision waiting for review, with Request changes and Approve at the bottom right](/images/guide/en/review-list.webp)

## Submit for review

1. Choose **Review** in the sidebar, then **Drafts**.
2. Choose your revision. Check what it changes.
3. Choose **Submit for review**.

The revision is now **In review** and can no longer be edited. The reviewers who may review all of
it hear about it. [Your first edit](/guide/getting-started/first-edit) walks through this once.

While it is a draft, **Take out of this revision** beside a changed item puts that item back as it
is on main. A new module is deleted, a deleted one comes back. This cannot be undone.

## Request changes

1. Open the revision under **Waiting**.
2. Write in **Review comment** what should change.
3. Choose **Request changes**.

The revision goes back to its author as a draft, and every approval it had is cleared. Without a
review comment the button stays greyed out: **Say in the review comment what should change.**

## Approve

1. Open the revision under **Waiting**. Read its changes; see
   [Compare the changes](/guide/review/compare).
2. Optionally write a **Review comment**.
3. Choose **Approve**.

Some revisions need a sign-off as well, for a restricted module type. The button is then **Sign
off**. See [Approvals and sign-offs](/guide/review/approvals). Once every approval is given, the
revision moves to **Approved**.

You never approve, sign off or return a revision you wrote, or wrote part of: **You wrote this
revision, or part of it: someone else reviews it.** A small team can change that; see
[Approve your own revision](/guide/review/self-approval).

## Merge

1. Choose **Approved**, then the revision. The panel says **Ready to merge into main**.
2. Choose **Merge**.

Its changes are now in main and go into the next release. If something was pushed to the
revision's branch after it was submitted, the revision goes back to its author instead: **Its
branch changed after it was submitted, so it went back to its author.**

## More in the panel

- **Rename**: its author changes the title and the description while it is open.
- ⋯ › **Show in audit log**: an administrator sees everything that happened to it.
