The project settings, the terms and the page designs live in the content, beside the modules. A
change to them goes into a revision and through the same review: nothing applies until it is
merged.

## Make the change in a revision

1. Choose or start a revision. An administrator changing the project settings sees **Changes are
   saved into the revision “…” and reviewed before they apply.**
2. Change the settings, a term or a page design.
3. Submit the revision as any other; see [From draft to main](/guide/review/workflow).

**Open in review** above the settings goes straight to the revision's review.

## What the reviewer sees

Each such change is an item of its own, marked **Configuration**, **Term** or **Page design**, with
the file it is in, such as `openfolia.yaml`. **What changed:** lists the parts that changed, for example
`moduleTypes/procedure`.

![A revision changing the project settings: the item openfolia.yaml with What changed, and the approvals](/images/guide/en/review-approvals.webp)

These items look the same inline, side by side and rendered. Their changes count in the item's
chip as **~1** for each changed part.

## What they need

- A change to the project settings needs the review and every sign-off there is. See
  [Approvals and sign-offs](/guide/review/approvals).
- A term or a page design needs the review only.
- Only someone whose role covers all content may review them.

While one revision changes the project settings, another cannot: **Another revision is changing
the project settings. They can be changed here once it is merged or abandoned.**
