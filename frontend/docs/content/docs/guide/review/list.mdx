**Review** in the sidebar lists revisions by where they stand. Choose one to see its changes in the
middle and its approvals on the right.

![The review page: the list on the left, the changes in the middle, the approvals on the right](/images/guide/en/review-list.webp)

## Choose what the list shows

At the top right, choose one of three:

- **Waiting**: submitted, waiting for review or a sign-off. The page title reads **Waiting for
  review**.
- **Approved**: approved, waiting for a publisher to merge them: **Approved, waiting to merge**.
- **Drafts**: not submitted yet.

The number beside each says how many revisions it holds, so an empty **Waiting** never hides a
draft. You see only revisions you may read.

## Read a row

Each row shows the revision's title, who started it and when it last changed, and its state, such
as **In review**. **Contributor** marks a revision by someone whose role may write but neither
review nor approve.

Under the title of the chosen revision, a line sums it up, for example **1 changed item · English
· compared with main**, and the checks next to it: blocking issues and warnings. Choose them to
open [the checks](/guide/checks/run-checks).

## Page through

The list shows 20 revisions at a time. **Rows per page** changes that, **Previous** and **Next**
turn the page. The address keeps the filter, the page and the chosen revision, so a link to it
opens the same view.

On a narrow screen the page shows one part at a time. Switch with **Revisions**, **Changes** and
**Approvals** above it.
