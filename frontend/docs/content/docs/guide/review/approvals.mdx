The **Approvals** card on the right of the **Review** page lists what a revision needs, in order,
and who gave it. A ring marks what it waits for now, a tick what is given.

![Approvals: the review given, a sign-off for procedures waiting, then the merge](/images/guide/en/review-approvals.webp)

## What a revision needs

- **Review**: always. A reviewer gives it with **Approve**.
- **Sign-off: Procedure** (or another type): for every restricted module type among the modules it
  changes. An approver of that type gives it with **Sign off**.
- **Merge**: once everything above is given. A publisher merges it.

A change to the project settings needs every sign-off there is. A module type is restricted when
an administrator marks it **Restricted** under **Admin** › **Project settings** › **Module types**.
A type restricted after the revision was submitted needs its sign-off too.

## Read a line

- **Waiting for a reviewer**, **Waiting for an approver of this type**: what it waits for now.
- **Needed once it is submitted**: the revision is still a draft.
- **Approved by Sample Reviewer**, with when.
- **Into main once approved**, then **Ready to merge into main**.

## Who may give what

- The same person may give the review and a sign-off.
- Nobody approves, signs off or returns a revision they wrote, or wrote part of. Someone who
  carried a fix forward or moved it is not its only author: the fix's writer counts too. See
  [Approve your own revision](/guide/review/self-approval) for the exception.
- A sign-off needs an approver whose role covers every changed module of that type.

## When an approver loses their role

An approval counts only while its approver still has the role that gave it. If an administrator
takes the role away, or narrows it, the line says **Approved by Sample Reviewer, which no longer
counts: they lost the role**, and the step waits again.

Someone else with the role can then give it. Until then **Merge** is refused: **An approval it needs
no longer counts: its approver lost the role. Return it to be reviewed again.** A reviewer returns
it with **Request changes**, and the author submits it again.

[Roles](/guide/reference/roles) lists which role may review, approve and merge.
