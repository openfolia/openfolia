By default every revision is reviewed by someone other than its author: four eyes. A team of one or
two may not have a second person for every review. An administrator can then let authors approve
their own revisions.

## Turn it on

1. Choose **Admin**, then **Project settings**.
2. Under **Installation**, choose **Review**.
3. Under **Who may approve their own revision**, choose one:
   - **Nobody: four eyes**: the default.
   - **Its author may give the review**: a restricted type's sign-off still needs someone else.
   - **Its author may give the review and sign-offs**: an author takes their own revision to
     approved alone.

![Project settings, Installation, Review: the three choices, with Its author may give the review chosen](/images/guide/en/review-self-approval-setting.webp)

The choice is saved at once and applies to the whole installation. It is not part of a revision.
Authors still need the rights to review and approve everything their revision changes. Approvals
already given stay when the choice changes back.

## Approve your own revision

With self-approval on, the panel of your own revision in review says **Four eyes is off for this
installation: you may approve your own revision. Sign-offs still need someone else.**

1. Write a **Review comment** if you like.
2. Choose **Approve**.

The **Approvals** card then shows the badge **Approved by its author**, and the step reads
**Approved by its author, Sample Admin**. The audit log marks the approval **By its author**.

![An approved revision with the badge Approved by its author in the Approvals card](/images/guide/en/review-self-approved.webp)

An author never sends their own revision back with **Request changes**, whatever the choice.

## Who counts as an author

Four eyes and self-approval apply to everyone who wrote the revision, not only the person who
started it:

- Everyone whose save points are in it.
- The writer of a fix that someone else carried forward to main or moved onto a new main. Their
  fix stays theirs, even when the carrier typed it again by hand.
- Not someone whose changes reached the branch outside the editor, such as a push to the content
  repository. Those changes name nobody.

If you count as an author, **Approve**, **Sign off** and **Request changes** are greyed out for you:
**You wrote this revision, or part of it: someone else reviews it.**
