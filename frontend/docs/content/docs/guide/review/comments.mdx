In the review, a comment sits on the block it is about, in a thread others can reply to. The
revision's author and everyone who may review it can comment, while it is a draft, in review or
approved.

![A reviewer's comment under the paragraph it is about, with Edit, Delete, Reply and Resolve](/images/guide/en/review-comments.webp)

## Add a comment

1. Find the block in the changes. Every changed block has **Comment** at its top right.
2. Choose **Comment** and type in **New comment**.
3. Choose **Comment** below the box, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>
   <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a Mac).

<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels. With the keyboard, move to a change with <kbd>j</kbd> or
<kbd>k</kbd> and press <kbd>c</kbd> to comment on it. In the **Rendered** view,
**Comment** is beside each change in the list above the page.

Your comment shows at once and is sent in the background. If it could not be sent, it says **Not
sent**: choose **Try again** or **Discard**.

## Reply and resolve

- **Reply** opens **Your reply** under the thread.
- **Resolve** marks the thread done. It folds to one line marked **Resolved**; **Reopen** opens it
  again.

## Edit or delete your own

Only your own comments show **Edit** and **Delete**.

- **Edit** turns the comment into a box. Change it and choose **Save**. The comment then says
  **edited** beside its time.
- **Delete** removes it at once. The message **Comment deleted.** offers **Undo** for a few
  seconds. A first comment that has replies leaves **Comment deleted** above them, so the replies
  still make sense.

## When the block is gone

A later save point may remove the block a comment was on. The comment stays, under **A comment on a
removed block** at the end of the module, marked **Its block is no longer there**.
