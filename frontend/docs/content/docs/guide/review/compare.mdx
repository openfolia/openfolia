The middle of the **Review** page compares each changed item with main: manuals, modules, the
configuration, terms and page designs. Choose how to see the changes in **Show the changes**.

## Find your way through the changes

- **Changed items**, the chips above the changes, list every item with its counts, such as **+4**.
  Choose one to jump to it.
- The marks: **+** added, **−** removed, **~** changed, **↕** moved. Inside a changed text, new
  words are underlined in green and old words struck through in red.
- The arrows, or <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd> (also <kbd>n</kbd> and <kbd>p</kbd>), move to the
  next and the previous change and show where you are, for example **2 of 6**.
- Five items show per page; **Next** turns to the rest.

## A manual: its outline

A changed manual shows its **Outline**: chapters and modules, each one added, removed or moved
marked. Unchanged entries fold away, for example **5 unchanged entries · show**. **Show its
changes** beside a module jumps to that module's own changes.

![A revision's changes: the manual's outline with a module added, then a changed module](/images/guide/en/review-outline.webp)

## A module: inline

**Inline** is the default. Each changed block says what changed, for example **Paragraph added** or
**Safety notice text changed**. Unchanged blocks fold away; choose **3 unchanged blocks · show** to
read them. A module whose title, type, audience or other details changed lists them under
**Changed details**.

## A module: side by side

**Side by side** shows **Before** on the left and **After** on the right, block beside block. A
block added on one side leaves a hatched gap on the other, so the two stay level.

![Side by side: the module before and after, with the added paragraph and a gap on the left](/images/guide/en/review-side-by-side.webp)

A replaced image offers **Compare by swiping** and **Show side by side**. A moved block offers
**Show where it was** and **Show where it is now**.

## A module: rendered

**Rendered** shows the new version as readers see it, with the changes marked on the page. Above
it, **Changes in this module** lists them; **Show on the page** scrolls to one. Choose the variant
at the top: a change that this variant leaves out is counted, not shown.

![Rendered: the page as readers see it, with the added paragraph listed above it](/images/guide/en/review-rendered.webp)

Manuals, the configuration, terms and page designs look the same in every view. On a phone, side by
side is not offered.
