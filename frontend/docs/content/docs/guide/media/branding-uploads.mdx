The branding's logos and icons are images of their own, kept apart from the content's media. They
are uploaded under **Admin**, **Branding**, and count towards the storage limit like every other
file.

## Upload a logo or an icon

1. Choose **Admin** in the sidebar, then **Branding**.
2. Under **Logos and icons**, choose **Upload** beside a field: **Logo**, **Logo on dark pages**,
   **Mark**, **Favicon** or **App icon**. **Replace** changes one that is set.
3. Choose the file. The image shows in the field and everywhere it is used, for everyone on their
   next load.

![The Logos and icons card: the logo, the mark and the icons, each with Upload](/images/guide/en/media-branding.webp)

The file must be:

- a PNG or WebP. SVG is not taken: export it as PNG.
- at most 1 MB and 4096 pixels a side.
- one still picture. An animated PNG or WebP is refused: **The image must be one still picture:
  an animated PNG or WebP is not taken.**
- square, for the **Favicon** and the **App icon**.

A refused file is not kept, and the field keeps what it had. The reason shows under the field.

## Images no field uses

**Remove** takes an image out of its field; the field shows the default again. An image no field
uses is removed on its own a day after its upload. Uploading the same file again stores nothing
new.

The branding holds a limited number of new images. When it is full, the editor says **The branding
holds as many new images as it can (200). Images no field uses are removed a day after their
upload.** Wait a day, then upload again.

A branding image counts towards the installation's [storage limit](/guide/media/storage-usage). Past
the limit, the upload is refused.

For the product name and the colours, see [Branding](/guide/admin/branding).
