A safety notice can show symbols, such as the general warning sign. The editor shows each symbol's
artwork beside the signal word, in the menu where you choose it, and in the preview. The PDF prints
the same artwork.

## Choose a symbol

1. In a safety notice, choose the button at its top right (**Safety notice level and symbols**).
2. Under **Symbols**, each symbol shows its artwork beside its name. Choose one or more.

![A safety notice with the general warning sign, and its menu with the levels and the symbols](/images/guide/en/media-safety-symbols.webp)

The notice shows the artwork at once. Screen readers read the symbol's name.

## A symbol without artwork

A symbol can be set up before its artwork is there. The notice then shows its name and **No
artwork**. The preview leaves it out, and the PDF prints a grey placeholder in its place. Add the
artwork, or choose another symbol.

## Where symbols come from

An admin decides which symbols there are, under **Admin**, **Project settings**, **Safety
symbols**: each has an ID and a name. The artwork is a file named after the ID, an SVG or a PNG,
kept in the content repository's `symbols` folder by whoever looks after the repository. The page
design's safety standard sets the symbols' shapes and colours.
