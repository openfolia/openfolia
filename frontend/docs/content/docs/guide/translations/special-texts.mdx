Most text lives in modules. A few texts live elsewhere and are translated the same way: they show
in the translation status, go out in a delta export and come back with an import.

## Page design text

A page design's free text, such as a subtitle or an imprint on the back page, is a row of its own
in the **Status** tab. It says **Page design** under its name.

1. Choose **Translations** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the cell of a page design, for example Classic in the DE column.
3. The details list each text that needs work, with the source and the translation. Placeholders
   such as `{product-name}` stay as they are.
4. **Open the page design** opens it, to translate a text there yourself.

![The details of the page design Classic in German: its title page texts](/images/guide/en/translation-special-texts.webp)

A page design without a text in a language prints the source language's text there. A manual's
checks report this.

## Callout legends

The legend of an image callout belongs to its module. It is translated with the module's other
blocks and needs nothing extra.

## Manual text

A manual's title, the titles of its groups and its cover image's alternative text (for screen
readers) are a row of their own, marked **Manual text**. Its details list each text: **Title**,
**Group title**, **The manual’s cover image** or the cover image of a variant. **Open the manual**
opens the manual. You can also type a translation there yourself: see
[Manual settings](/guide/manuals/settings).

## Project labels

The labels of the project settings are one row for the whole project, **Project labels**, at the
top of the table: module types, audiences, facets and their values, variant attributes and their
values, text attributes and safety symbols. PDFs and the portal show them in the manual's
language, and the editor in each person's interface language. Its details list each label;
**Open the project settings** opens them, to type a translation yourself: see
[Project settings](/guide/admin/project-settings).

A label that is the same in every language, such as a product name, still counts as untranslated
until a translation brings it back unchanged.

## Export and import them

Page designs, manual texts, the project labels and modules go in exports of their own. Choose the
rows of one kind in the **Status** tab, then **Create delta export**. The line above the table
names what you chose, for example **1 page design chosen** or **Project labels chosen**. The
import puts the texts back into the design, the manual or the project settings; exporting
and importing the labels need the right to change the project settings.

When the source text changes, the manual's checks say how many texts of the page design or of the
manual need translating again. The
[translation report](/guide/translations/reports) counts these texts too.

## Who may change a translation record

A page design, a manual and the project settings each keep a record of the source text every
translation was made from.
The import writes it. Only a person who manages translations into a language may change that
language's entry. This holds for every way a change reaches a revision: saving, a save point,
**Update from main**, carrying a revision forward and submitting.

If a change by someone else would alter the entry of a language they do not manage, it is
refused and nothing is saved. The editor says **The request was not valid.** Keep the record as
it is, or ask a translation manager.

Taking the page design or manual out of the revision (**Take out of this revision** in
**Review**) is always allowed: it puts back the record as main has it.
