A cross-reference points the reader to another place in the documentation: a section, a figure, a
table, a procedure or one of its steps. It can point into this module or into another one.

## Add a cross-reference

1. Put the caret where the reference should go, for example after "see ".
2. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `cross`, and choose **Cross-reference**.
3. Choose the target. Type in **Find a target…** to narrow the list.

![The Cross-reference picker: the sections of this module, and Another module…](/images/guide/en/writing-cross-reference.webp)

The list has these groups:

- **In this module**: its sections, figures, tables, procedures and steps. Each says its kind, such
  as **Section** or **Figure**.
- One group for each module this module includes with a module reference. **Whole module** links
  to that module itself.
- **Elsewhere**: **Another module…** lets you choose any module, then its target.

The reference shows in the text as a chip with its target's words, such as a section's title.

## Change or remove a cross-reference

- To point it elsewhere, click the chip and choose another target.
- To remove it, put the caret after it and press <kbd>Backspace</kbd>.

## When the target is gone

When its target or its module is deleted, the chip is marked. Pointing at it says why:
**Broken cross-reference: its target is no longer in …**, or **Broken cross-reference: the module …
can't be found**. Choose a new target.

The checks also report a cross-reference whose target is missing, or is not in the manual being
built. See [Run the checks](/guide/checks/run-checks).
