Terms are the words your manuals use for things: the preferred word in each language, the variants
that are allowed or forbidden, and a definition. Modules link to terms, and a manual with a glossary
lists the terms it links.

![The Terminology page with the terms and the details of a deprecated term](/images/guide/en/terminology.webp)

## Find a term

1. Choose **Terminology** in the sidebar. The table shows each term in every language.
2. Narrow the list with **Language**, **Domain** and **Status**, or type in **Search terms and
   definitions**.
3. Choose a term to open its **Term details** beside the list.

## Add a term

You change terms in a draft revision, as modules. See
[Start a revision](/guide/change-sets/start-a-revision).

1. Choose **New term**.
2. Enter the **Preferred term** and its **Language**. The **ID** is made from it and cannot be
   changed later.
3. Optionally, enter a **Domain**.
4. Choose **Add term**.

Then fill in the details: **Allowed variants** and **Forbidden variants** (type a word and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>), and the **Definition**, per language. Each change is saved at
once in the revision.

## Deprecate a term

1. Open the term.
2. Set **Status** to **Deprecated**.
3. Under **Replaced by**, choose the term to use instead, or leave **No replacement**.

A module that still links a deprecated term gets a warning in its checks: **Deprecated term**,
naming the replacement. See [Run the checks](/guide/checks/run-checks).

## Delete a term

Choose **Delete term** in its details, then **Delete term** again. The toast offers **Undo**.

A term that a module links, or that another term names as its replacement, cannot be deleted. The
details say which modules or terms keep it.

## Term links and the glossary

A term link marks a word in a module's text as the term. In the editor it is underlined thinly,
and pointing at it shows the definition, as in the preview. To link a term while you write, accept
its suggestion with **Tab**, or choose **Term** in the **/** menu (see
[Terminology and style](/guide/checks/writing)). A manual set up with a glossary lists every term it
links at the end, in its web pages and its PDF, and each term link leads there. Your admin can
rename the glossary's heading in the output labels.

## Export the terms

Choose **TBX** or **CSV** at the top of the page. The file holds the terms as the revision you
chose reads them: TBX for translation tools, CSV for a spreadsheet.
