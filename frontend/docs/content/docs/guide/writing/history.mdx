title: 'A module’s history'
description: 'See every version of a module, compare two of them, and restore an older one as a draft.'

---

Every save point makes a new version of a module. Its history lists them, shows what each one
changed, and brings an older one back.

## Open the history

1. Open the module.
2. Choose **History** in the side panel. It lists the versions, newest first.
3. Choose a version, or **Open history** for the newest.

![A module's history: the versions on the left, what version 2 changed on the right](/images/guide/en/module-history.webp)

Each version names who made it, when, and in which revision. **Released** marks a version in a
release. Without access to drafts, you see only the released versions.

## Compare two versions

The history compares the version you chose with the one before it.

1. Choose a version in the list.
2. To compare with another one, choose **Compare with** under that version, for example
   **Compare with version 1**. It is marked **Compared**.
3. Switch the view with **Inline**, **Side by side** or **Rendered**, as on the review screen.

## Restore an older version

1. Choose a draft revision at the top: the version is restored into it.
2. In the history, choose the version to bring back.
3. Choose **Restore … as draft**, for example **Restore version 1 as draft**.

The module now reads as that version, in your revision, ready to edit and review like any other
change. The toast offers **Undo**. The newest version cannot be restored: it is the module already.

A deleted module keeps its history. In **Content**, turn on **Show deleted** and choose the module:
its history opens. Restore a version that has its content.
