## With the block actions menu

1. Point at a block. A grip (⠿) appears to its left.
2. Choose the grip. Or put the caret in the block and press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>F10</kbd>.
3. Choose what to do:
   - **Move up** or **Move down**: one place up or down.
   - **Duplicate**: a copy below it.
   - **Save as snippet**: makes reusable wording of it. See [Snippets](/guide/writing/snippets).
   - **Delete**: removes it. Undo brings it back.

![The block actions menu open beside a paragraph](/images/guide/en/block-menu.webp)

## By dragging

Drag a block by its grip to its new place. A line shows where it lands. You can drag a block out of
a notice or a list, too.

## With the keyboard

- <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move the block
  with the caret (<kbd>⌥</kbd> on a Mac). The arrows in the toolbar do the same.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> indent and outdent a list
  item.
