Callouts are numbered markers on a picture. The legend says what each number is, and the text can
refer to a number: "Press the power switch (2)".

## Add callouts

1. Under the image, choose **Edit callouts**.
2. Click the picture where the callout should point. A numbered marker appears there, and its legend
   field opens.
3. Type the legend, such as "Power switch", and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
4. Add the next one the same way. **Add callout** puts one in the middle of the picture instead.
5. Choose **Done**.

![Callouts being edited: the markers on the picture, the list of legends, and the preview with the legend](/images/guide/en/writing-callouts.webp)

## Move or delete a callout

While you edit the callouts:

- Drag a number or its anchor point to a new place.
- Or select a marker and move it with the arrow keys; with <kbd>Shift</kbd> in larger steps.
- Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> on a selected marker, or choose the bin in its row, to delete it. **Undo**
  in the message brings it back.

An image has at most 100 callouts.

## Change the order of the numbers

The callouts are numbered in reading order, from the top of the picture down. To number them your
own way, drag a row of the list by its grip (or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> on it). The list then says **Numbered in the order set here.**
**Number in reading order** goes back.

## Show a callout for some variants only

1. In the list, choose the callout's legend. Its settings open below it.
2. Choose **Variants** and tick the variants it is for, then **Apply**.

Without a choice it says **Shown in every variant**. A callout hidden in a variant shows "–"
instead of a number, and the others close up. The header says which variant the numbers are for:
**numbered across all variants**, or numbered as in the variant the preview shows.

**Leader line** in the same place sets the number off from its point, joined by a line.

## Refer to a callout in the text

1. Put the caret in the text, for example after "Press the power switch ".
2. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `callout`, and choose **Callout reference**.
3. Choose the callout by its number and legend.

The reference shows the callout's number and follows it when the numbers change. A reference marked
"?" has no number: **The callout is gone: its image or the callout was removed**, or **Hidden in the
chosen variant: it has no number there**. Click it to choose another callout.

## See what belongs together

Point at a marker, a row of the list or a reference: the others for the same callout light up. The
preview does the same with its markers, legend entries and references.

## In the outputs and in translations

The preview and the PDF draw the markers on the picture and the legend under or beside it. A legend
beside an image that is longer than the image is high gets smaller type, then is cut off; the checks
then report **Callout legend cut off**. Shorten the legend.

The legends are text like any other: they go to translation with the module, and come back in its
other languages. See [Export for translation](/guide/translations/export).
