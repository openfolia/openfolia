**Content** lists every module of the revision you are reading.

![The Content page: groups on the left, filters on top, the modules in a table](/images/guide/en/content-browser.webp)

## Find a module

1. Choose **Content** in the sidebar.
2. Narrow the list in one or more ways:
   - Choose a group on the left. **All modules** shows every one; the other groups are the module
     types, such as **Procedure** or **Safety**.
   - Type in **Search titles and IDs**. Press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump there.
   - Use the filters above the list: **Language**, **Status**, **Audience**, **Type**, **Keyword**,
     and the ones your project adds (in the sample content, **Region**).
3. Choose a module's title to open it.

## Read the list

- **Status** shows whether the module is released or still a draft.
- **Languages** shows a badge per language with the state of its translation: a filled dot
  means **Up to date**, a ring **Outdated**, a half-filled ring **At agency** and a dashed circle
  **Not translated**. The legend below the list names each one. If your role does not show the
  translation status, a thin ring says only **Translated**: the language version is there.
- **Variants** shows which variants the module is for: **All**, or only some.

## Deprecated and deleted modules

The list leaves them out. Turn on **Show deprecated** or **Show deleted** to see them too.

## Many modules

The list shows a page at a time. Choose **Next** and **Previous** below it, and how many
**Rows per page**. The filters and the page stay in the address, so a link shows the same list.
