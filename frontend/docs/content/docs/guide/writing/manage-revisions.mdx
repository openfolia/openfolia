A revision collects your changes. You can rename a revision you started until it is merged. While it is a draft, you can also take
a single change out of it without touching the rest.

## Rename a revision

1. Switch to the revision in the revision menu at the top right.
2. Open the revision menu again and choose **Rename revision**.
3. Change the **Title**, and add a **Description** if you like.
4. Choose **Save**.

## Take a change out of the revision

Each module, manual, term or project file the revision changes is one change. Taking one out
discards everything the revision did to it, saved or not.

1. Choose **Review** in the sidebar, with your draft revision chosen.
2. Find the change in the list.
3. Choose **Take out of this revision** on it.
4. Read what happens, then choose **Take out**:
   - created in this revision: it is deleted, with everything written in it;
   - changed in this revision: it goes back to how it is on main;
   - deleted in this revision: it comes back as it is on main.

![Take out confirmation for a module created in the revision](/images/guide/en/revision-take-out.webp)

This cannot be undone. Only the person who started the revision takes changes out, and only while
it is a draft.

To bring back an older version of one module instead, see [A module's
history](/guide/writing/history).
