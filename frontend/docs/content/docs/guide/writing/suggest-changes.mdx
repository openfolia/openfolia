Some roles may not edit content directly but may suggest changes, for example someone in service
who spots a wrong value. A suggestion is a revision of its own with one module in it. A reviewer
checks it, like any revision.

## Suggest a change to a module

1. Open the module from **Content**. It opens read-only: **Read-only: suggest a change to edit
   it**.
2. Choose **Suggest a change** at the top right. A revision named after the module is started, and
   the module opens for editing in it.
3. Make your change in the text.
4. Under **Your suggestion**, fill in **What did you change?**: what you changed and why. Reviewers
   read this first.
5. Choose **Submit for review**.

![A suggestion: the module open for editing, Your suggestion with its note and Submit for review](/images/guide/en/module-suggest.webp)

The module is read-only again: **Read-only: your suggestion is in review**. Once approved, it
waits to be published with the next merge.

## Suggest a new module

In **Content**, choose **Suggest a new module**. It is suggested in a revision of its own, named
after it, and opens in the editor. Write it, then submit it as above.

## Who can suggest

Your role decides whether you edit or suggest: see [Roles](/guide/reference/roles). Someone who
may edit can use **Suggest a change** too, as a short cut: it starts a revision for that module and
chooses it.
