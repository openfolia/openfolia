A safety notice warns of a hazard: what it is, what happens, and how to avoid it. A note sets a
remark that is not about safety apart from the text.

## Add a safety notice

1. Put the caret where the notice should go.
2. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `saf`, and choose **Safety notice**. It is also in the **Insert** menu,
   under **Notices**.
3. Fill in its three parts:
   - **Hazard**: the danger in a few words, such as "Heavy battery".
   - **Consequence**: what happens if the reader ignores it.
   - **Measure**: what to do to avoid it. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for more lines.

A new notice is a warning without symbols.

## Set its level and symbols

1. Choose the arrow at the top right of the notice (**Safety notice level and symbols**).
2. Under **Level**, choose danger, warning, caution or notice. The notice takes the level's colour
   and signal word.
3. Under **Symbols**, tick each safety symbol the notice shows. Tick it again to take it off.

![A warning with its symbol, the menu open with the levels and the symbols](/images/guide/en/writing-safety-menu.webp)

The signal words are in the module's language: a German module shows GEFAHR where an English one
shows DANGER. Your administrator sets the words and the symbols you can choose from.

## Safety symbols and their artwork

Each symbol shows its artwork beside the signal word, in the editor, the preview and the outputs.
A symbol without artwork shows its name, marked **No artwork**. The preview and the outputs leave
it out until someone adds the artwork. See
[Safety symbol artwork](/guide/media/safety-symbols).

## Add a note, a tip or something important

1. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `note`, and choose **Note**.
2. Type the text.
3. To change its kind, choose the arrow beside its label (**Kind**) and choose a note, a tip or
   something important.

A heading cannot go inside a note or a safety notice.

## Gather the safety notices

A safety leaflet collects the safety modules of a manual. See
[Make a safety leaflet](/guide/manuals/safety-leaflet).
