A module you no longer want as it is can get a new ID, be deprecated, or be deleted. Each is a
change in your draft revision, reviewed like any other. See
[Start a revision](/guide/change-sets/start-a-revision).

![A module's More actions menu: Show in audit log, Deprecate… and Delete…](/images/guide/en/module-actions.webp)

## Change a module's ID

The ID names the module in manuals and in other modules. Changing it rewrites all of them.

1. Open the module and choose **Details** in the side panel.
2. Choose **Change ID** beside the module's ID.
3. Enter the **New ID**: lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens, for example
   `battery-charging`.
4. Check what moves: the line under the field names the languages of the module it moves, and
   the files naming it that it rewrites, such as manuals.
5. Choose **Change ID**.

![Change ID open in the Details: the new ID and the two manuals it rewrites](/images/guide/en/module-change-id.webp)

The toast offers **Undo**. The ID cannot change while someone edits the module, or a module or
manual naming it, in another revision.

## Deprecate a module

A deprecated module stays readable and stays in the manuals that include it, but they are warned.
It takes effect once the revision is merged.

1. Open the module, in its source language.
2. Choose **More actions** (**…**) at the top right, then **Deprecate…**.
3. Enter a **Reason**: one line, shown wherever the module appears as deprecated.
4. Optionally, choose the module that replaces it under **Replaced by (optional)**.
5. Choose **Deprecate**.

The module now shows **Deprecated** with its reason and replacement. In **Content**, turn on
**Show deprecated** to list deprecated modules. To take it back, choose **More actions**, then
**Undo deprecation**.

## Delete a module

1. Open the module and choose **More actions**, then **Delete…**. Or, in **Content**, choose **…**
   at the end of the module's row, then **Delete…**.
2. Under **What to delete**, choose **Only the … version** for one language, or **The whole
   module, in every language**.
3. Read **Where it is used**: the manuals and modules that still use it. It can still be deleted;
   the checks then report these uses until they are fixed.
4. Choose **Delete module**, or **Delete … version** for one language.

The toast offers **Undo**. Undo works while the revision is a draft. Published content keeps the
module until the revision is merged. A deleted module keeps its history: see
[A module's history](/guide/writing/history).
