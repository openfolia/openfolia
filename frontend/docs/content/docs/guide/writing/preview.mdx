## Beside the editor

1. Choose **Preview** at the top right of the module editor.
2. The preview opens beside the text. It follows what you type, even before it is saved: the
   heading then says **with unsaved changes**.

![The preview beside the editor, with a note on what it hides for this variant](/images/guide/en/module-preview.webp)

The note above the preview names what is **Hidden for this variant**: blocks meant for other
variants. Choose **Show hidden blocks** to see them too, marked.

Choose **Close preview** (×) to close it.

## Full size

Choose **Open in the full preview** (the arrow above the preview). The **Preview** page opens. There
you choose:

- **What to preview**: **This module**, or the **Whole manual**.
- **Language** and **Variant**.
- **Output**: the **Portal** page or the **PDF**.

**Back to editor** takes you back.
