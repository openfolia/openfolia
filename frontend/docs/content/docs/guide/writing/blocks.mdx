A module is made of blocks: paragraphs, headings, lists, tables, notices, procedures, images. Two
menus add them. Both list the same blocks.

## With the Insert menu

1. Put the caret where the block should go.
2. Choose **Insert** in the toolbar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>
   (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a Mac).
3. Type in **Find a block…** to narrow the list, or scroll through its groups: **Content**,
   **Notices** and **Structure**.
4. Choose the block.

![The Insert menu with the block search and the Content group](/images/guide/en/insert-menu.webp)

A block that cannot go at the caret is greyed out and says why, for example **Can't go at the
caret**.

## With the slash menu

1. Start an empty line and type <kbd>/</kbd>.
2. Type a few letters to filter, for example `/tab` for **Table**.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or choose the block.

![The slash menu below the caret with Text, the headings, the lists and Table](/images/guide/en/slash-menu.webp)

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the menu and keep the slash as text.

## Fill in the block

Most blocks are ready to type into. Some ask for more first:

- **Safety notice:** fill in the hazard and the measure. The button at its top right sets its
  **Level** (danger, warning, caution) and its **Symbols**.
- **Procedure:** a title and numbered steps. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the next step.
- **Module reference** and **Snippet:** choose what to include. See
  [Reuse a module](/guide/writing/reuse-modules) and [Snippets](/guide/writing/snippets).
- **Variant block:** choose which variants get it. See
  [Text for some variants](/guide/variants/variant-blocks).

The [block types](/guide/reference/block-types) lists every block and what it is for.
