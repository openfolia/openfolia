An image block shows a picture from the media library, at a size you choose, with an alt text and
an optional caption.

## Insert an image

1. Put the caret where the image should go.
2. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `ima`, and choose **Image**. It is also in the **Insert** menu.
3. In **Insert image**, choose a picture. Type in **Search images by alt text, caption or module** to
   find one. Or choose **Upload** to add a PNG or JPEG from your computer: it is uploaded into your
   revision and chosen.
4. Choose its **Size in documents**: **Icon**, **30%**, **50%**, **75%** or **Full**.
5. Type its **Alt text · …** (in the module's language). It is required.
6. Type a **Caption · optional** if the image needs one. It shows under the image.
7. Choose **Insert**.

![The Edit image dialog: the pictures on the left, size, alt text and caption on the right](/images/guide/en/writing-image-dialog.webp)

**Insert** stays greyed out until a picture is chosen and uploaded; the line beside it says so.
Without an alt text, the dialog marks the field and inserts nothing.

## Write a good alt text

The alt text says what the image shows, for someone who cannot see it. Screen readers read it, and
it stands where the image cannot show. Describe what matters for the task, such as "The charger from
the front, with its switch and lamp", not "Image".

Each language's module has its own alt text and caption: translate them with the text.

## Change an image

Choose **Edit** under the image, or double-click it. The **Edit image** dialog opens with the image's
settings. Choose **Apply**. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> takes the change back.

## What an image block says

Under the picture, the block shows its caption and its alt text. Instead of the picture it can say:

- **No image chosen**: choose **Edit** and pick one.
- **Image not found, or not visible to you**: the file is gone, or in content you may not read.
- **The image could not be loaded**: choose **Retry**.
- **No alt text: required**: choose **Edit** and type one.

If the server refuses a file when saving, the editor says which image and offers **Upload again**.

## Callouts

Numbered markers on the picture, with a legend, are callouts. See
[Image callouts](/guide/writing/image-callouts). The [media library](/guide/media/library) lists
every image.
