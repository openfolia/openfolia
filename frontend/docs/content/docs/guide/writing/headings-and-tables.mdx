---
title: 'Headings, lists and tables'
description: 'Divide a module with headings on three levels, and hold its text in lists and in tables with merged cells and aligned columns.'
articleId: 'guide.writing.headings-and-tables'
---

Headings divide a module into sections. A **Heading 2** goes inside a **Heading 1**, a **Heading 3**
inside a **Heading 2**. Lists and tables hold the text between them.

## Add a heading

1. Put the caret in a line of text.
2. Open the **Text style** menu in the toolbar. It names the style at the caret, such as **Text**.
3. Choose **Heading 1**, **Heading 2** or **Heading 3**.

Or start an empty line, type <kbd>/</kbd> and choose a heading. A level that cannot go at the caret
is greyed out and says why, for example **Needs a heading at its level before it to go under.**

![The Text style menu in a Heading 2, with Level down greyed out and Level up](/images/guide/en/writing-heading-levels.webp)

The **Outline** on the left lists the headings as H1, H2 and H3.

## Move a heading a level up or down

1. Put the caret in the heading.
2. Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> for **Level down**, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> for **Level up**.
   Both are also at the end of the **Text style** menu.

The text under the heading and its subheadings move with it. Headings go three levels deep at
most. A heading cannot stand in a list, a table, a variant block or a safety notice.

## Make a list

1. Choose **Bulleted list** or **Numbered list** in the toolbar, or type <kbd>/</kbd> and choose one.
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the next item. <kbd>Enter</kbd> in an empty last item ends the list.
3. Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to put an item under the one before it, <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> to
   bring it back.

## Make a table

1. Type <kbd>/</kbd>, then `tab`, and choose **Table**. A new table has two rows and two columns;
   the first row is the header row.
2. Type into the cells. <kbd>Tab</kbd> goes to the next cell, <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> to the
   one before. <kbd>Tab</kbd> in the last cell adds a row.
3. For rows and columns, choose **Table actions** in the toolbar. The button is there while the caret
   is in a table.

![The Table actions menu: header row, rows, columns, merging and alignment](/images/guide/en/writing-table-menu.webp)

In **Table actions**:

- **Header row** turns the first row into the header row, or back.
- **Insert row above**, **Insert row below**, **Insert column before** and **Insert column after**
  add a row or a column beside the caret's cell.
- **Delete row**, **Delete column** and **Delete table** remove them.

A table cannot hold a table.

## Merge cells

1. Put the caret in the first cell.
2. In **Table actions**, choose **Merge with next cell** or **Merge with cell below**.

**Split cell** takes a merged cell apart again. While a table has merged cells, you cannot add or
remove rows and columns: the menu says **Split the merged cells to add or remove rows and columns.**

## Align a column

1. Put the caret in a cell of the column.
2. In **Table actions**, under **Column alignment**, choose **Left**, **Centre** or **Right**. Scroll
   the menu to reach it.

The whole column follows, in the editor and in the outputs.
