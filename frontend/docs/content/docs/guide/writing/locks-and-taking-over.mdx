When you open a module in a draft revision, it is locked for you: the header says **Locked by
you**. Nobody else can change it until your revision is merged or abandoned. So two people never
overwrite each other's work.

## When someone else is editing it

Open a module someone else holds, and the editor says so: **Someone else is editing this module**.
It names them, since when, and their revision.

![The dialog Someone else is editing this module, with Take over editing, Back and Open read-only](/images/guide/en/module-locked.webp)

Choose one:

- **Open read-only**: read the module now. Edit it once they are done.
- **Back**: go back to where you came from.
- **Take over editing**: only when it cannot wait, for example when a colleague is away.

## Take over editing

1. Choose **Take over editing**.
2. Say **Why are you taking over?** This is required, and goes into the audit log.
3. Choose **Take over editing** again.

Their editor turns read-only, and they are told. What they saved stays in their revision. You may
need a role that allows taking over.

## When you hold the module in another revision

The editor says **You're editing this module in another revision**. Choose:

- **Open that revision** to go on there.
- **Release it there**, to edit it here instead. This works only while nothing of it has changed
  there.
