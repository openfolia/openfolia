With two-factor sign-in, signing in takes a code from your phone, besides your password or the code
we email you. Someone who learns your password then still cannot sign in.

It is for people who sign in here, with a password or a code by email. If you sign in with your
organisation's account (single sign-on), your organisation's sign-in service asks for its own second
factor, and the account menu does not offer this one.

## Turn it on

You need an authenticator app on your phone: any app that shows six-digit codes which change every
30 seconds.

1. Open the account menu and choose **Two-factor sign-in**.
2. Enter your password, if you have one, and choose **Set up**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app. If you cannot scan it, enter the key beside it in the app.
4. Enter the code the app shows and choose **Turn on**.
5. Save your ten recovery codes: **Copy codes** or **Download**. They are shown only now.

Your other sessions end; this one goes on.

## Sign in

1. Sign in as usual. The page **Two-factor sign-in** then asks for a code.
2. Enter the six digits the app shows. You can paste them, with or without a space.

Each code works once. The second step stays open for ten minutes, in the browser you started in.
Then the page says **Sign in again**. An invitation, a reset link and a code by email ask for it
too.

After too many wrong codes the page says how long to wait.

## Sign in without your phone

1. On the page **Two-factor sign-in**, choose **Use a recovery code**.
2. Enter one of your recovery codes. Each works once.

No codes left either? Ask your admin to reset your two-factor sign-in.

## Make new recovery codes or turn it off

1. Open the account menu and choose **Two-factor sign-in**. It says how many recovery codes are
   left.
2. Choose **New recovery codes** or **Turn off**.
3. Enter your password, or a current code.

New codes replace the earlier ones. To move to a new phone, turn it off and set it up again.

## When it is required

Your admin can require it. If you do not have it yet, the page **Set up two-factor sign-in** comes
after your password at your next sign-in. You are signed in once it is on and your recovery codes
are shown. If you turn it off, you set it up again at your next sign-in.
