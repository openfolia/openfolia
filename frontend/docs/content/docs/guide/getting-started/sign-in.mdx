Open the editor's address in your browser. The **Sign in** page shows the ways your installation
offers. Your admin decides which ones there are.

## Sign in

Use the way your page shows:

- **With your organisation's account:** choose **Sign in with …** (the name of your organisation's
  sign-in service). Sign in there. You come back to the editor.
- **With a password:** enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.
  Forgot it? Choose **Forgot password?** and follow the link in the email. The link works once,
  for one hour.
- **With a code by email:** choose **Email me a code** and enter your email address. Type the
  six-digit code from the email, or open its link. The code works once, for a few minutes (10 unless your admin set another time).
  Some installations sign in only this way: the page then shows **Email me a code** alone.
- **As a demo user** (demo installations only): choose a person under **Demo users**. Each one has
  other roles, so you can try what an author, a reviewer or an admin sees.

After signing in you see **Content**, or the page you asked for.

## Set your password from a link

An invitation or a reset link opens the page **Welcome, …** or **Choose a new password**.

1. Enter a **New password**: at least 12 characters. A few unrelated words make a good one.
2. Enter it again under **Repeat the password**.
3. Choose **Set password and sign in**.

You are signed in at once. After a reset, your other sessions end. A link works once: if the page
says **This link does not work** or **This link has run out**, ask your admin for a new one, or use
**Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## Change your password

Shown only when you signed in with a password.

1. Open the account menu and choose **Change password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**, then the **New password**, and again under **Repeat the new
   password**.
3. Choose **Change password**.

Your other sessions end; this one goes on.

## Sign out

1. Open the account menu: choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Sign out**.

A demo installation has no **Sign out**. Use **Switch user** in the account menu instead.

## When you cannot sign in

Ask your admin. They can send you a new invitation or a link to set a new password, or reactivate
your account.
