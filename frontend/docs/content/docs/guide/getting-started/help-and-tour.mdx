## The page for this screen

Every screen has a question mark beside its title. Choose it to open the page of this user guide
that explains the screen, in a new tab and in the language you use the editor in.

The account menu has the same link: open the menu at the foot of the sidebar and choose **Help
for this page**.

## Find a guide page

The search palette finds pages of this guide too, by their titles and summaries.

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac).
2. Type what you want to do, for example `xliff`.
3. The pages that match are under **Help**, marked **User guide**. Open one with <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Every keyboard shortcut

Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere outside a text field, or choose **Keyboard shortcuts** in the account
menu. The list shows each shortcut where it works, with the keys of your computer.

Close it with <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

The [keyboard shortcuts](/guide/reference/shortcuts) page lists the same shortcuts for Windows,
Linux and macOS.

## The tour

The first time you open the editor, a short tour shows you around: **Content**, the editor,
revisions, **Review** and the search. Each step points at the part of the screen it explains.

![The first step of the tour beside Content in the sidebar](/images/guide/en/tour.webp)

- **Next** and **Back** move between the steps; **Done** on the last one ends the tour.
- **Skip** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> ends it at once.

The tour comes once. To see it again, open the account menu and choose **Take the tour**.

## Tips

The first time you come to a part of the editor, a tip may explain it: a short note beside the
button or list it is about, with **Learn more** when this guide has a page on it. Each tip shows
once.

![A tip beside New module on the Content page](/images/guide/en/tip.webp)

- **Got it** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it for good.
- **Turn off tips** turns off every tip and the tour.

A tip never opens while a dialog, a menu or the search palette is open, or while you type in a
field: it waits until you are done.

Your choices are kept with your account, so they follow you to another browser or computer.

### Turn tips on or off

Open the account menu and choose **Show tips**: the check mark shows whether tips are on. **Reset
tips** turns them on and shows every tip again, also the ones you closed.

## When there are no help links

An administrator can point the help links at another copy of this guide, or turn them off for an
installation with no way out to the internet (`CCMS_DOCS_URL`, [self-hosting](/self-hosting)).
Without them, the question marks and **Help for this page** are not there, and the search
palette finds no guide pages. **Keyboard shortcuts** is always there.
