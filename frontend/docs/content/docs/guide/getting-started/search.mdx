## Search everything

The search palette finds modules, manuals, pages of the editor and actions, from any page.

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac), or choose **Search**
   in the sidebar.
2. Type a few letters of a title, a keyword or the text.
3. Move with <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd>, and open a result with <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

![The search palette with results for a search term](/images/guide/en/search-palette.webp)

The palette searches titles, keywords and text in every language you may read. A result found in
the text says so (**Text match**). Before you type, it lists what you opened recently.

Close it with <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## Search the list you are on

Lists such as **Content** and **Users** have their own search box. Press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to
it, then type. The filters beside it narrow the list further.
