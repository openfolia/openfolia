The bell beside the revision menu holds your notifications: revisions submitted to you, returned,
approved or published, locks taken over, releases and translation imports. See
[Find your way](/guide/getting-started/find-your-way#notifications).

You choose which of them you get, and which also come by email.

## Open your notification settings

1. Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Notifications**.

The page lists every kind of notification, in groups. Your choices are yours: an administrator
cannot change them for you.

## Choose per kind

For each kind, pick one of:

- **Off**: not in the bell, not by email. What is in the bell already stays there.
- **In the app only**: in the bell, no email.
- **App + email at once**: in the bell, and an email when it happens.
- **App + daily summary**: in the bell, and once a day one email with everything of that day.

What each kind starts with:

| Group               | Kind                                             | Starts with         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Review and workflow | A revision is submitted to you for review        | App + email at once |
| Review and workflow | Your revision is returned with changes requested | App + email at once |
| Review and workflow | Your revision is approved                        | App + email at once |
| Review and workflow | Your revision is signed off                      | App + email at once |
| Review and workflow | Your revision is published                       | App + email at once |
| Review and workflow | An administrator broke one of your locks         | In the app only     |
| Releases            | A release is published                           | App + daily summary |
| Releases            | A release’s file that failed is ready after all  | App + daily summary |
| Translations        | Your translation import is done                  | App + daily summary |
| Translations        | Your translation import failed                   | App + daily summary |
| Admin alerts        | A release’s file failed to build                 | App + email at once |
| Admin alerts        | A backup failed                                  | App + email at once |
| Admin alerts        | No good backup for longer than expected          | App + email at once |

**Admin alerts** reach only people whose role has the permission for them. You can turn one off,
but the page then says what you will miss.

One thing is not for choosing: when the
[emergency account](/guide/admin/single-sign-on) is used, every administrator finds it in the bell
and gets an email, whatever they set.

## Pause or turn off everything

Two switches at the top of the page:

- **Pause all email**: the bell keeps everything, and no email is sent. Your choices per kind are
  kept, so turning it back on restores them.
- **Turn off all notifications**: nothing in the bell and nothing by email. It applies to you
  only.

Neither stops the notice of the emergency account's use (above).

## The daily summary

The kinds set to **App + daily summary** come together in one email a day.

- It comes at the hour you pick, in your time zone. The time zone starts as your browser's, and
  the hour as 08:00 unless your installation sets another.
- On a day with nothing to report, nothing is sent.
- It is written in your editor language, English or German, like every email.

## What an email holds

Each email has a clear subject, one button that opens the item in the editor, a line that says
why you get it, and a link to unsubscribe.

Several changes to the same item within about two minutes come as one email. At most 20 emails
come at once in an hour (your installation may set another number); the rest wait for the daily
summary.

## Unsubscribe

Choose **Unsubscribe** in your mail program, or the link at the end of the email. You need not
sign in. The link opens a page that names the kinds and asks first: choose
**Stop these emails**.

Unsubscribing stops only the kinds that email was about, and only the way it came: the link in a
daily summary stops the summary for them, the link in an email at once stops the email at once.
Those kinds then come **In the app only**. Nothing else changes, and you can turn the email on
again on the **Notifications** page.

## When no email comes

- **Email is off on the server.** The page says so and why. Your choices still apply to the
  bell, and nothing waits to be emailed later.
- **Your account has no email address.** The page says so too.
- **You lost access.** Access is checked again when the email goes: if you can no longer read
  that revision, you get no email about it.
- **You reached the hourly limit.** The rest come in the daily summary.
- **Email is paused**, or the kind is set to **In the app only** or **Off**.

  shows whether it is on and the latest emails that failed.
</ServerSetting>
