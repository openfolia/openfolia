## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left leads to every area: **Content**, **Manuals**, **Review**,
**Translations**, **Releases**, **Reports**, **Media**, **Terminology** and **Admin**. You see only
the areas your role opens.

- To make it narrow, choose the button beside the logo, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>
  (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> on a Mac). The sidebar then shows icons only.
- **Search** at the top opens the search palette. See [Search](/guide/getting-started/search).

A page your role does not open says **No access** and names the permission it needs. Ask your admin
for it, or choose **Go to the start page**.

## The revision menu

The menu at the top right says what you are reading: **Published content**, or the name of a
revision. Choose it to switch to another revision or to start one. See
[Start a revision](/guide/change-sets/start-a-revision).

## Notifications

The bell beside the revision menu shows how many notifications you have not read. Choose it to
open them. You hear about revisions submitted to you, returned or approved, locks taken over and
releases. To choose which you get, and which also come by email, see
[Notifications](/guide/getting-started/notifications).

## The account menu

Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

![The account menu with theme, language and the demo users](/images/guide/en/account-menu.webp)

- **Theme:** **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your computer. When your computer is set
  to reduce motion, the editor does without its animations.
- **Language:** the language of the editor itself, **English** or **Deutsch**. The content keeps its
  own languages.
- **Help for this page** and **Keyboard shortcuts**: the user guide's page for the screen you are
  on, and every shortcut ([help in the editor](/guide/getting-started/help-and-tour)).
- **Show tips**, **Take the tour** and **Reset tips**: the tips and the first-run tour, on or off
  ([the tour and tips](/guide/getting-started/help-and-tour#tips)).
- **Licence:** the editor's licence and a link to its source code.
- **Sign out**, or **Switch user** on a demo installation.

## Links keep what you see

The address in the browser holds the page, the revision, the filters and the open panels. Copy it
to send a colleague exactly what you see. They need the right to read that revision.
