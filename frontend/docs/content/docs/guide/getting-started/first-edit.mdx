This page takes you through one small change: you start a revision, change a module and submit
it for review. You need a role that may edit content, such as **Author**.

## 1. Start a revision

1. Open the revision menu at the top right. It shows **Published content**.
2. Choose **New revision**.
3. Enter a **Title** that says what you change, for example "Update the battery instructions".
4. Choose **Start**.

![The revision menu with a new revision being started](/images/guide/en/revision-new.webp)

The menu now shows your revision. Everything you change goes into it.

## 2. Open a module

1. Choose **Content** in the sidebar.
2. Find the module: type in **Search titles and IDs**, or choose a group on the left.
3. Choose the module's title.

The module opens in the editor. The header says **Locked by you**: nobody else can change this
module until your revision is done.

## 3. Change the text

1. Click into the text and type.
2. Watch the header: it shows **Saving…**, then **Saved**. There is no save button.

See [Edit a module](/guide/writing/edit-a-module) for everything the editor does.

## 4. Submit for review

1. Choose **Review** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Drafts**, then your revision. You see every change it makes.
3. Choose **Submit for review**.

![The review page of a revision, with Submit for review at the bottom right](/images/guide/en/review-submit.webp)

The revision is now **In review**. The reviewers hear about it. When a reviewer approves it, a
publisher merges it into the published content. If the reviewer requests changes, it comes back to
you as a draft: change what they asked for and submit it again.
