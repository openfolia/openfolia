This section is for developers who connect other systems to the released content: a portal, an
app, a search index or an AI assistant. Writing and reviewing do not need it. Start with
[The Delivery API](/guide/integrations/delivery-api).

The [API reference](/api) has a page per route, generated from the server, and both OpenAPI
documents to download. Two guides cover a whole integration:
[Integrating with a RAG pipeline](/guides/rag) and [the MCP server](/guides/mcp) for AI
assistants. A release output as an [iiRDS package](/guide/releases/iirds) or a
[DITA project](/guide/releases/dita) is under Releases.

In the editor, an admin sets up what integrations use: service accounts and their tokens, and
webhooks. See [Admin](/guide/admin).
