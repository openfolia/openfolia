`GET /search` finds modules, manuals and terms among what the reader may see, at `?ref=` like
every content read. It is part of the [Delivery API](/api/delivery) and needs `content.read`.

## Search

```sh
curl -gs -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  "https://docs.example.com/api/search?filter[q]=battery&filter[lang]=en&page[size]=20"
```

- **`filter[q]`**: 1 to 200 characters; at most 8 words are used. Case and diacritics do not
  matter (`ä` is `a`, `ß` is `ss`), and every word has to match.
- **What it looks in:** a module's title, keywords, FAQ questions and text; a manual's title; a
  term's preferred, allowed and forbidden variants and its definition.
- **Filters**, all optional and all of them required to hold: `filter[lang]`,
  `filter[resourceType]` (`Module`, `Manual` or `Term`), and for modules `filter[type]` (a module
  type), `filter[audience]`, `filter[facet]=<facet>:<value>` and `filter[keyword]` (the last two
  may repeat). With a module filter, `filter[q]` may be left out: then every module the filters
  let through is a result, by title.
- Paged like every list (`page[number]`, `page[size]`). Any other parameter is 400
  `unsupported-parameter`.

## Results

Each result is a `SearchResult`: `attributes` hold `resourceType`, `resourceId`, `lang`, `title`
and, when text matched, `blockId` (the block, step or FAQ entry it matched in); `relationships`
point at the resource. `meta` holds:

- `matchedOn`: `title`, `keyword`, `question`, `text`, `meaning`, or `filter` without a query;
- `excerpt`: plain text, never markup;
- `matches`: `{ start, end }` offsets into the excerpt, so you highlight by slicing the text and
  never insert HTML.

Results come in order of what matched: the title first, then keywords, questions and text. A
result never includes content outside the reader's scope, and `meta.page.total` counts only what
they may see.

## Word forms, typos and meaning

With the default Postgres search (`CCMS_SEARCH_ADAPTER=postgres`), nothing to set up:

- **Word forms** in the module's language: `starting` finds `starts`.
- **Typos** in titles and keywords.
- **Meaning**, when the operator turned on semantic search: a reader's words find a module that
  says the same in others. Without it, no text leaves the server. See
  [Semantic search](/guide/self-hosting/semantic-search).

`CCMS_SEARCH_ADAPTER=memory` matches words alone.

## Limits

| Answer                 | When                                                                                                    | Setting                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 429 `rate-limited`     | A reader asked for more searches a minute than allowed (every page counts), or ran more than 2 at once. | `CCMS_SEARCH_PER_MINUTE` (120)     |
| 503 `database-timeout` | One search spent its time budget in the database.                                                       | `CCMS_SEARCH_TIMEOUT_MS` (5000 ms) |
| 503 `search-busy`      | The server already runs as many searches as allowed, and none finished within 2 s.                      | `CCMS_SEARCH_CONCURRENCY` (4)      |

Each carries `Retry-After`: wait that long and ask again. The limits hold per server, so behind
several servers a reader gets as many times the rate. Never a partial page: a search answers in
full or not at all.
