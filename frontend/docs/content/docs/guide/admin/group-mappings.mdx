description: 'Give the members of a group from your identity provider a role, and take it away again.'

When people sign in through an identity provider, the provider tells the editor which groups they
are in. A group mapping gives every member of a group a role. A person's groups are the ones the
provider sent at their last sign-in.

## Add a mapping

1. Open **Admin**, then **Group mappings**.
2. Choose **Add mapping**.
3. Under **Group**, type the group's name exactly as the identity provider sends it. Upper and
   lower case count.
4. Under **Role**, choose the role.
5. Choose **Add mapping**.

![The Add group mapping dialog with a group name and the role still to choose](/images/guide/en/admin-group-mappings.webp)

Members who signed in through the group before hold the role at once; the others from their next
sign-in. The same group and role twice is refused.

Until an identity provider is set up for sign-in, the page says so. You can add mappings already:
they apply once people sign in with one.

## See which groups give a role

The **Role** filter above the list shows the mappings of one role. From **Roles**, **Show the
groups that give this role** opens the list filtered to that role.

A role held through a group is marked with the group symbol on the **Users** page and under **Who
holds it** on the **Roles** page: **Through a sign-in group**. To change it, change the mapping,
not the person.

## Remove a mapping

1. Choose **Remove** in the mapping's row, and confirm.
2. The group's members lose the role at once, unless it was given to them directly.

A removal that would leave nobody who can manage roles is refused: **This mapping stays**. Give a
role with **admin.roles** to someone directly first, then remove the mapping.
