Branding replaces the product's own name, logos and colours with yours, in the editor and on the
sign-in page. Without it, the default branding shows. Changes apply at once; everyone else sees
them on their next load. It needs a role with **admin.branding**.

![The Branding page with the product name, the logos and icons, and the preview](/images/guide/en/admin-branding.webp)

## Name the product

1. Open **Admin**, then **Branding**.
2. Under **Product name**, type the **Name**, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or leave the field.

It shows in the browser tab, next to the logo and on the sign-in page. Leave it empty for the
default name.

## Upload logos and icons

Under **Logos and icons**, choose **Upload** beside the image to set, and pick a PNG or WebP file
of at most 1 MB and 4096 pixels a side. SVG is not taken: export it as PNG.

- **Logo**: with the name, for light pages.
- **Logo on dark pages**: without it, the light logo shows there too.
- **Mark**: compact, for the collapsed sidebar.
- **Favicon**: the browser tab's icon. Square, at least 32 × 32 pixels.
- **App icon**: the icon a phone puts on its home screen. Square, at least 180 × 180 pixels.

**Replace** and **Remove** change or drop one image. An animated image or an icon that is not
square is refused, and what was saved stays. The uploaded images are listed in the media library:
see [Branding uploads](/guide/media/branding-uploads).

## Set the colours

1. Under **Colours**, type the **Primary colour** as `#` and six hex digits, or pick it, then press
   <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
2. Set an **Accent** if you like: it tints the sidebar on light pages.

Buttons, links and the focus ring take the primary colour; dark pages use a lighter shade of it.
The **Contrast checks** show each pair of colours on light and dark pages. A colour that leaves too
little contrast is not saved. Safety and status colours never change.

**Use default** takes one colour back to the default. **Preview** shows the result on a light and
a dark page.

## Go back to the default branding

Choose **Reset to default**, then **Reset**. The name, logos, icons and colours go back to the
defaults, for everyone.
