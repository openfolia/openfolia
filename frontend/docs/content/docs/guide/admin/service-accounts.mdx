A service account is a machine client, such as a portal build, a pipeline or an agent. It holds
roles like a person, and calls the API with tokens of its own. A token is shown once, when it is
issued.

## Add a service account

1. Open **Admin**, then **Service accounts**.
2. Choose **New service account**.
3. Fill in **Name** and, if you like, **Description**. The **Service account ID** is made from the
   name. It cannot be changed later.
4. Under **Roles**, choose its roles. Roles that manage access (**admin.roles**) are not offered.
5. Choose **Add service account**.

## Issue a token

1. In the service account's row, choose **Tokens**.
2. Choose **Issue token**.
3. Under **What it is for**, say where the token goes, for example "Portal build, production". It
   shows in the list and in the audit log.
4. Under **Expires after**, choose how long it lasts: 30, 90, 180 or 365 days.
5. Choose **Issue**.
6. **Copy the token now**: choose **Copy token** and store it where its client reads it. Then
   choose **Done**.

![The tokens of a service account, with a new token shown once to copy](/images/guide/en/admin-service-token.webp)

The token cannot be shown again, not even here. If it is lost, issue a new one and revoke the old
one.

The list shows each token's name, its expiry, when it was last used, and its state: **Active**, **Expired** or
**Revoked**. Issue one token for each client that calls the API, so you can revoke one without
stopping the others.

## Revoke a token

Choose **Revoke** beside the token, and confirm. Every client using it is refused from its next
request on. A revoked token cannot be restored.

## Deactivate a service account

Choose **Deactivate** in its row, and confirm. None of its tokens can act any more, and every lock
it holds is released. Its roles and tokens stay: **Reactivate** brings back the tokens that are
neither revoked nor expired.
