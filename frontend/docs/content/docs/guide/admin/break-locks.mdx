---
title: 'Free the locks someone holds'
description: 'Take over one module, release every lock of a person who left, or break all locks of a revision.'
articleId: 'guide.admin.break-locks'
---

Whoever edits a module holds its lock, so nobody else changes it at the same time. A lock stays
while its holder's revision is open. When the holder is away or has left, an admin can free it.

## One module

Open the module and choose **Take over editing**. You say why, the other person is told,
and their editor turns read-only. See [Locks and taking over](/guide/writing/locks-and-taking-over).

## Every lock of a person who left

Deactivate the person in **Admin › Users** (see [Users and roles](/guide/admin/users-and-roles)).
Every lock they hold is released, and the message says how many. A service account's locks are
released the same way, when it is deactivated.

## Every lock of a revision or a holder, at once

The editor has no button for this. A tool or script with an admin's access can do it through the
API (`POST /locks/break`, with a comment): it breaks every lock of one revision or of one holder,
and each broken lock is recorded in the [audit log](/guide/admin/audit-log) as **Lock broken**.
Holders are told. See the [API reference](/api).
