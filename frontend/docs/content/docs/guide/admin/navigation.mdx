**Admin** in the sidebar opens the administration. Its pages are listed in a rail on the left,
under five headings. Every page is one click away.

## The rail

| Group                 | Pages                                                          |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **People and access** | **Users**, **Roles**, **Group mappings**, **Service accounts** |
| **Content**           | **Project settings**, **Variants**, **Page design**            |
| **Look**              | **Branding**                                                   |
| **Integrations**      | **Webhooks**                                                   |
| **Operations**        | **System**, **Backups**, **Audit log**                         |

![The Users page with the admin rail and its five groups on the left](/images/guide/en/admin-users.webp)

The open page is marked in the rail, and its name is the heading. A page's main action, such as
**New user** or **Add subscriber**, sits at the top right.

**Project settings** has a second list of its own: one topic per page. See
[Project settings](/guide/admin/project-settings).

## Only the pages your role opens

The rail lists only the pages your role may open. A group with none left is left out. Someone who
may only manage webhooks sees **Integrations** with **Webhooks** alone, and **Admin** opens that
page.

A link to a page your role cannot open shows **No access** and names the permission it needs.
[Roles and permissions](/guide/reference/roles) lists what each permission opens.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the rail becomes a select above the page, **Admin sections**. It lists the same
pages under the same group headings. Choose a page to open it.

## Open an admin page from the search palette

Every admin page and every project-settings topic is also in the search palette.

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac).
2. Type the page's name, for example `Languages` or `Webhooks`.
3. Under **Pages**, each result shows where it is, for example **Admin › Project settings ›
   Languages**. Choose it.

![The search palette listing two settings topics under Pages](/images/guide/en/admin-palette.webp)

The palette lists only the pages your role opens. See [Search](/guide/getting-started/search) for
the rest of the palette.
