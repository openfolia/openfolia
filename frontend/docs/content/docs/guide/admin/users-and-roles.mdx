Each person has a user and holds one or more roles. A role is a set of permissions, and a scope
says where they apply. Changes apply at once and are written to the audit log.

## Add a person

1. Open **Admin**, then **Users**.
2. Choose **New user**.
3. Fill in **Name** and **Email address**. The **User ID** is made from the address. It cannot be
   changed later.
4. Under **Roles**, choose the roles the person holds.
5. Choose **Add user**.

![The New user dialog with a name, an email address, the user ID and the roles](/images/guide/en/admin-user-new.webp)

The list shows each person's email address and ID under their name. It searches name, email and
ID (press <kbd>/</kbd>), and **All**, **Active** and **Deactivated** filter it. **Edit** and
**Deactivate** are at the end of each row; in a narrow window, scroll the list sideways to reach them.

## How a person signs in

How people sign in is set up on the server: with an identity provider, with a password, with a
code by email, or several of these. When sign-in with a password is on, the Users page shows more:

- A **Sign-in** column says how each person signs in: **Password**, **Provider**, **Password and
  provider**, **Invited, not yet signed in**, **Invite ran out** or **Not yet**.
- A new person gets an invite at once: a one-time link, shown once to copy. When email is set up,
  it is emailed to them too.
- **Invite** makes a new invite, **Withdraw invite** ends an open one, and **Reset link** makes a
  password reset link for someone who has a password. Each link works once, for a limited time,
  and replaces any earlier one.

A person without an email address gets no link. Codes by email need no work here: the person asks
for one on the sign-in page.

## Make or change a role

1. Open **Roles**. Choose a role on the left, or **New role**.
2. Fill in **Role name** and **Description**, and tick its **Permissions**.
3. Under **Scope**, limit where they apply: the **Module types**, the **Audience levels**, the
   **Languages** and each facet. A line left at **All** applies to all of it.
4. Choose **Save changes**, or **Create role** for a new one.

![The Roles page with the Author role, its permissions, its scope and who holds it](/images/guide/en/admin-roles.webp)

**admin.roles**, **admin.branding** and **webhook.manage** always apply everywhere. Under **Who
holds it**, add or remove people and service accounts. A role with **admin.roles** cannot go to a
service account. **Delete role** works once nobody holds it.

[Roles and permissions](/guide/reference/roles) lists every permission.

## Deactivate a person

1. In **Users**, choose **Deactivate** in the person's row, and confirm.
2. The person can no longer act. Approvals they gave stop counting, and every lock they hold is
   released. Their roles stay.

**Reactivate** brings them back.
