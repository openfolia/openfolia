A webhook subscriber is a URL that is told when something happens, for example when a manual is
released. Each call is signed JSON, and failed calls are retried. It needs a role with
**webhook.manage**.

## Add a subscriber

1. Open **Admin**, then **Webhooks**.
2. Choose **Add subscriber**.
3. Fill in the **URL**: an `https://` address that answers with a 2xx status. Give it a **Name**.
4. Under **Events**, tick what it is told about, such as a manual released or a release withdrawn.
5. Under **Audience limit**, choose up to which audience it hears about content, or **No limit (every audience its owner may read)**.
6. Choose **Add subscriber**.
7. Copy the **Signing secret** and give it to the receiver: it checks the signature of every call
   with it. You won't see this secret again. Then choose **Done**.

A subscriber hears only what its owner may read: the person who added it, or who last changed its
URL.

## Custom headers

For a receiver that checks requests its own way, add headers under **Custom headers** in the same
dialog: **Add header**, then a **Name** and a **Value**. Turn on **Secret** for a value such as a
token: it is stored encrypted and afterwards shows only as **Set**. **Replace** changes it.

When the URL changes, enter each secret value again. A secret is never sent to a receiver it was
not given for.

## Test and read the deliveries

Choose a subscriber to see its recent deliveries beside the list.

![A subscriber and its recent deliveries, after a test event the receiver did not answer](/images/guide/en/admin-webhooks.webp)

1. Choose **Send test event**. A test event (ping) is sent at once, also to a paused subscriber,
   and is never retried.
2. The result shows above the list: delivered with the receiver's status code, or failed and why.
3. Open a delivery to see its attempts, the **Headers sent** and the **Payload**.

Filter the deliveries by result (**Delivered**, **Failed** and the others) and by event.

## Retry, pause and fix a subscriber

- A delivery that failed is retried automatically with backoff. **Retry** in a failed delivery
  sends it again at once.
- A subscriber that keeps failing is turned off: **Failing**. Fix the receiver, then choose
  **Re-enable** on its card or in its **⋯** menu.
- The **⋯** menu also holds **Edit**, **Pause** and **Resume**, **Rotate secret** and **Delete**.

**Rotate secret** makes a new signing secret, shown once. The old one is still sent beside it for a
day, so the receiver can switch over.
