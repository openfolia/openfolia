Your directory (the identity provider that holds your staff) can keep the editor's users and
groups up to date with SCIM 2.0. It creates a user when someone joins, changes their name and
email, and deactivates them when they leave. Its groups give roles through [group
mappings](/guide/admin/group-mappings).

The SCIM address of a hosted OpenFolia is:

```text
https://<team>.openfolia.com/api/scim/v2
```

Elsewhere, it is your editor's address followed by `/api/scim/v2`.

## Make a SCIM token

The directory signs in with the token of a service account that may do nothing but provision.

1. Open **Admin**, then **Roles**, and choose **New role**.
2. Name it, for example "Directory", and tick only **scim.provision**. Choose **Create role**.
3. Open **Service accounts** and choose **New service account**. Give it the "Directory" role and
   choose **Add service account**.
4. In its row, choose **Tokens**, then **Issue token**, and copy the token.
5. In your directory's SCIM settings, enter the SCIM address above and the token as the bearer
   token.

Only a service account may provision: a person is refused, whatever roles they hold. Revoke the
token, or deactivate the service account, to stop provisioning at once. Without a token, the SCIM
address answers 401.

## What the directory can do

- **Users**: create, read, list, replace, change (PATCH) and delete. `userName` is required and
  unique, ignoring case. The name shown in the editor is the `displayName`, else the `name`, else
  the `userName`. The email is the primary one, else the work one, else the first.
- **Groups**: create, read, list, replace, change and delete, with their members. Members must be
  users the directory provisioned.
- **Filters and paging**: `filter` (for example `userName eq "jane@example.org"`), `startIndex`
  and `count` (at most 200), `attributes` and `excludedAttributes`.
- **Discovery**: `ServiceProviderConfig`, `ResourceTypes` and `Schemas`.

Sorting, bulk changes, ETags and passwords are not supported. Lists show only users and groups
the directory provisioned.

## When someone leaves

When the directory sets a user to inactive, or deletes them, the user loses access at once:

- every session ends, and their sign-in links and codes stop working;
- every lock they hold is released;
- their next request is refused.

Their revisions, comments and approvals history stay. Deleting a user in the directory
deactivates them in the editor and unlinks them from the directory; it never deletes them. Set
them to active again to bring them back; an update that leaves `active` out changes nothing
about it. An update that sets someone to inactive deactivates them whatever else in it is wrong;
if it is wrong, the rest of it may be left out. When the directory provisions someone it deleted
again with the same email address, the same user comes back, active, unless you reactivated or
deactivated them in the editor since. Someone you deactivated in the editor stays deactivated,
whatever the directory sends: reactivate them in **Users**.

A change that would leave nobody who can manage roles is refused, even a deactivation.

## Groups and roles

A provisioned group gives roles like a sign-in group: add a group mapping with the group's
`displayName` and a role. Members hold the role at once, and lose it when they leave the group,
the group is deleted or the mapping is removed.

A provisioned group never gives a role holding **admin.roles**, **admin.branding**,
**admin.backups**, **webhook.manage** or **scim.provision**, even when a mapping names it: the
SCIM token cannot make an admin. A role that gains one of those permissions is taken from the
members of provisioned groups at once. Give such roles to people directly, or through a sign-in
group.

## Users who are already there

When the directory creates a user whose email belongs to someone already in the editor, the
existing user is linked instead of a new one, as your server's setting for linking sign-ins by
email allows (`CCMS_OIDC_LINK_BY_EMAIL`: by default only someone who has never signed in). It
never links someone who is deactivated, or who holds a permission beyond reading, writing and
reviewing content (**content.read**, **content.read.draft**, **content.edit**,
**content.review**).

## Email addresses

SCIM changes the email address only of users it created, only until they first sign in, and only
while they hold no more than those permissions. After that, change the address in the editor,
under **Users**. Otherwise whoever holds the SCIM token could move an account to an address of
theirs and reset its password.

When someone's address changes in your directory, the directory's update is refused until you
have changed it in the editor too. Everything else goes through: other changes while the two
addresses differ because you changed the editor's first, and deactivating someone (but for the
last admin). An address you set in the editor stays until the directory's own changes.

Every change the directory makes is in the [audit log](/guide/admin/audit-log), under the service
account.
