title: 'Audiences'
description: 'Audience levels keep modules meant for a narrower audience out of a manual, and out of the reach of readers scoped to a wider one.'
articleId: 'guide.manuals.audiences'
---

An audience says who content is for. Your admin sets the levels in the project settings, from the
most open to the most restricted. In the sample content: **Public**, then **Internal**.

## Give a module its audience

1. Open the module and choose **Details** in the side panel.
2. Choose its **Audience**.

A module without an audience counts as the most restricted level. The **Details** tab names it:
**Not set: counts as Internal**; the Content list shows **Internal (not set)**.

The Content list's **Audience** filter finds modules by the level they count as. So
**Internal + not set** shows the internal modules and those without an audience. **Public** shows
only the public ones. Search and the API filter the same way.

## Give a manual its audience

Set the **Audience** in the manual's settings: see [Manual settings](/guide/manuals/settings). A
manual leaves out every module meant for a narrower audience than its own. With **Not set (every
audience)**, it leaves out none.

Say the operating manual is **Public** and one of its modules is **Internal**: the public manual
is built without it. The module stays in the outline, and the checks report it as **Above the
manual’s audience**, so nobody misses it.

![The manual's checks: Charging, set to Internal, is Above the manual's audience](/images/guide/en/manual-above-audience.webp)

To bring it back, give the module the manual's audience or a wider one, or remove it from the
outline. A safety leaflet follows the same rule: its source is a manual for the same audience or a
wider one. See [Make a safety leaflet](/guide/manuals/safety-leaflet).

## Readers scoped to an audience

A role can be limited to audience levels: **Up to** a level. Someone with that role reads only the
content meant for that level or a wider one; modules for a narrower audience stay hidden from
them.
Your admin sets this in the role's **Scope**: see [Users and roles](/guide/admin/users-and-roles).
