The **PDF preview** card in the manual builder shows two pages of the manual's PDF as it would be
printed now: the cover, and the first page after it (the contents, or the first chapter).

![The PDF preview card: the cover and the contents page, for the Small variant](/images/guide/en/manual-pdf-preview.webp)

## Read the thumbnails

1. Open the manual in **Manuals**. The card is on the right, below **Settings**.
2. With more than one variant build, choose the variant in the card's list.
3. The language is the one chosen in **Outputs**.

The thumbnails come from the real PDF, with the page design, the cover image and your latest edits
in the revision, even before a save point. After you change something, they render again a moment
later; until then the old ones stay, dimmed.

## Open the full preview

Choose the thumbnails to open the PDF preview of that variant. **Preview** at the top opens the
manual as a web page; its arrow offers the PDF too.

## Change the cover

The cover shows the manual's title and the page design's layout. Its image is chosen in the
manual's settings, for all variants or one: see
[Fonts and cover images](/guide/releases/page-design-assets). The layout itself is the page design:
see [Page designs](/guide/releases/page-designs).
