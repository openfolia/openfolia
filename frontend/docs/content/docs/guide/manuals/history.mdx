Every save point of a manual makes a new version: a module added or removed, the order changed, a
setting changed. Its history works like a module's.

## Open the history

1. Open the manual in **Manuals**.
2. Choose **…** at the top right, then **History**.

![A manual's history: the versions on the left, and what version 4 changed in the outline](/images/guide/en/manual-history.webp)

The versions are listed newest first, each with who made it, when and in which revision.
**Released** marks a version in a release.

## Compare two versions

Choose a version: it is compared with the one before it. The comparison shows the manual's details
and its outline: entries added, removed or moved. Unchanged entries are folded into a line such as
**4 unchanged entries · show**: choose it to open them. To compare with another version, choose
**Compare with** under it.

## Restore an older version

1. Choose a draft revision at the top.
2. In the history, choose the version to bring back.
3. Choose **Restore … as draft**, for example **Restore version 3 as draft**.

The manual reads as that version in your revision. The toast offers **Undo**.

A deleted manual has a history too: choose the manual's title at the top, turn on **Show deleted**
and choose it. See [Duplicate, rename, deprecate or delete a manual](/guide/manuals/manage).
