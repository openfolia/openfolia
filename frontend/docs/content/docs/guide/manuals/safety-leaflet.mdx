A safety leaflet holds the safety notices of a product's manual, on a few small pages. You do not
build it by hand: a derived manual gathers the modules of some types from its source manual, in the
source's order, each once. When the source changes, the leaflet follows.

## Create the leaflet

1. Choose **Manuals** in the sidebar, then the manual's title at the top, then **New manual**.
2. Enter a **Title**, for example "Safety leaflet for service staff".
3. Under **Kind**, choose **Derived from another manual**.
4. Choose the **Source manual**: the manual whose safety modules it gathers. A source is an
   assembled manual for the same audience or a wider one.
5. Leave **Module types it gathers** empty to gather the types your settings mark as safety
   content (in the sample content, **Safety**). Or choose the types.
6. Choose **Create and open**.

![The New manual dialog with Derived from another manual chosen](/images/guide/en/new-derived-manual.webp)

## Read the leaflet

The outline shows what the rule gathers: **3 modules, gathered from Operating manual**, say. Each
gathered module keeps the variant conditions it has in the source. A badge such as **Not in Small**
says that a variant does not get it.

![A safety leaflet: the gathered modules, and the Gathering rule beside them](/images/guide/en/safety-leaflet.webp)

You cannot drag or add rows here: the **Gathering rule** on the right decides the outline.

## Change the rule

In **Gathering rule**:

- **Source manual** and **Module types it gathers**: as when you created it.
- **Front matter (before the gathered modules)**: choose **Add** to put a module first, such as an
  introduction. Back matter works the same, after them.
- **Left out**: modules the leaflet does not gather. To leave one out, choose the crossed-out eye
  at the end of its row (or select it and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>). **Include again** brings it
  back.

The rule's checks stand beside it. **Blocking:** means the leaflet cannot be released until it is
fixed.

## Gather the safety notices instead

A leaflet can hold the safety notices of the source's modules rather than the whole modules:
every danger, warning, caution and notice, without the text around them.

1. In **Gathering rule**, under **What it gathers**, choose **Safety notices of the source’s
   modules**. (**Whole modules of the types below** is the default.)
2. Under **Group the notices**, choose **By source module** for a group per module, or **By hazard
   level** for a heading per level, most severe first.
3. Under **Hazard levels gathered**, untick a level to leave its notices out. Grouped by hazard
   level, drag a level to change the order of the groups.
4. Under **Module types it searches for notices**, choose the types to search, or leave it at
   **Every type**: every module of the source is searched, and the modules it refers to.

![The Gathering rule set to Safety notices, grouped by hazard level, with the levels gathered](/images/guide/en/manual-leaflet-notices.webp)

The outline then lists the modules holding notices, in the source's order: **3 modules holding
notices, gathered from Operating manual, grouped by hazard level**, say. Each notice keeps the
variant conditions it has in the source. A notice the rule leaves out (a module left out, a type
not searched, a level not gathered) is reported by the checks as **Safety notice left out**, so you
can confirm it is meant.

## Print it

The leaflet is a manual like any other: preview it, check it and release it. For small pages,
choose a leaflet page design in its **Settings** card, under **Page design**: A5 portrait, small
type, the modules running on. Your admin makes page designs under **Admin**, **Page design**.
