Everything here happens in a draft revision and is reviewed with it. Published content keeps the
manual as it is until the revision is merged.

![A manual's actions menu: Run checks, History, Rename, Duplicate, Deprecate… and Delete manual](/images/guide/en/manual-menu.webp)

Open the manual in **Manuals**, then choose **…** at the top right (**Actions for** the manual).

## Create a manual

Choose the manual's title at the top, then **New manual**. See
[Build a manual](/guide/manuals/build). To start from another manual's outline, tick **Start from
a copy of another manual** and choose the **Manual to copy**.

## Duplicate a manual

1. Choose **Duplicate**. The **New manual** dialog opens with **Copy of** the manual as its title
   and the manual to copy chosen.
2. Change the **Title**: the manual's ID is made from it.
3. Choose **Create and open**.

## Rename a manual

1. Choose **Rename**. The title at the top turns into a field.
2. Type the new title and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or leave the field. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

The manual's ID stays as it is.

## Deprecate a manual

A deprecated manual stays readable and can still be released. It is deprecated once the revision
is merged.

1. Choose **Deprecate…**.
2. Enter a **Reason**, and optionally the manual it is **Replaced by (optional)**.
3. Choose **Deprecate**.

The toast offers **Undo**. Later, **Undo deprecation** in the same menu takes it back.

## Delete a manual

Choose **Delete manual**. It is deleted at once, in this revision, and the builder moves on to the
next manual. The toast offers **Undo** while the revision is a draft.

A deleted manual keeps its history. Choose the manual's title at the top, turn on **Show deleted**
and choose it: its history opens, where you restore a version. See
[A manual's history](/guide/manuals/history).
