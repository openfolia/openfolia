A manual puts modules in an order. Its outline lists them, numbered as the manual will be. Work in a
draft revision: see [Start a revision](/guide/change-sets/start-a-revision).

![The manual builder: the outline in the middle, the outputs on the right](/images/guide/en/manual-builder.webp)

## Open or create a manual

1. Choose **Manuals** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the manual's title at the top to switch to another manual. Type in **Search manuals** to
   find one.
3. To create one, choose **New manual** in the same list. Enter a **Title**, check the
   **Audience**, keep **Assembled from modules** as the **Kind**, and choose **Create and open**.

## Add modules

1. Choose **Add module**.
2. Type in **Search modules**, then choose the module. It is added after the selected row, or at
   the end.
3. No module fits? Choose **New module…** to create one and add it in one go.

![Add module open over the outline, with a search field and the modules](/images/guide/en/manual-add-module.webp)

## Put them in order

- Drag a row by its grip (⠿) to its new place.
- Or select a row and press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves a row into the chapter above, <kbd>Shift</kbd>
  <kbd>Tab</kbd> back out.

The keyboard button beside the module count lists every key. So does the
[keyboard shortcuts](/guide/reference/shortcuts) page.

## Chapters and row actions

Choose ⋯ at the end of a row:

- **Put into a new chapter**: the row moves into a new chapter in its place. Give it a **Title**
  and choose **Create chapter**.
- **Rename chapter**: change a chapter's title.
- **Only for some variants…**: include the row for some variants only. The row then shows them,
  such as **Model: Large**.
- **Remove**: takes the row out of the manual. **Undo** brings it back. The module itself stays.

## See what it builds

- **Outputs** lists each language and whether its portal page and its PDF are **Complete**,
  **Partial** (some modules fall back to the source language) or **Not translated**. Below, it says
  for which variants it is built.
- **Preview** opens the manual's web preview. The arrow beside it offers the **PDF** per variant
  and language.

## Settings

The **Settings** card on the right sets what the manual is built for: its **Audience**, its
**Variant builds** (each combination of variant values is built and checked as its own manual),
its **Text attributes** and its **Page design**. Choose **Edit settings**. Changes save at once in
the revision.

## Rename, duplicate, delete

Choose ⋯ at the top right for **Run checks**, **History**, **Rename**, **Duplicate** and
**Delete manual**.
